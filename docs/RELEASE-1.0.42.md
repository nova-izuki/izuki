# Izuki 1.0.42

Faster answers, apps that read what their connections can't, a friendlier face, a fuller Quick setup, and recent chats on the phone.

## Faster — no more minute of "thinking"
- A brain that's out of credits (or has a bad key) is left out for six hours instead of being asked again on every message — that alone cost seconds each time.
- A connected app that hangs now gives up in 20 seconds instead of 45.
- The status screen's buttons show "On it: Tidy my PC…" the moment you tap them, and a second tap no longer sends the same request again (several at once were slowing everything down).
- "Do it in the background" now really stays in the background — the AI can no longer hand it to the screen.

## Apps can do more
When an app's connection can't do something — LinkedIn notifications and messages, an Instagram or X feed — Izuki opens that page in its own browser, where you're signed in, and reads it for you, instead of saying the app "doesn't have" it.

## A friendlier hologram face
The Hologram face is redrawn like an animated character: a round face, big shiny eyes with a sparkle, a smile at rest, rosy cheeks and a soft mouth that talks — warm light, no dark eye sockets.

## Quick setup
A clear **Back** button (Esc works too), a progress bar, and more to set up from one place: talking hands-free, your TV, linking your phone and TV, the browser extension, buddy mode, colours and orb, and the tour — each button takes you straight to the right setting. The window can be dragged by the dim edge while Quick setup, What's New or the tour is open, and the list scrolls all the way up.

## Phone
- **Recent chats**: "New chat" keeps the old one; the home screen lists your recent chats — tap to pick one up again, × to delete it, or clear them all.
- **Later list on the phone**: "remind me later I'm buying milk" works on the phone by itself, even with the PC off, and shows on the home screen.

## The phone and TV apps update themselves
The Android phone and TV apps now check for a newer version and show **Update Izuki** — one press downloads it and opens Android's installer (Android asks once to allow it). Every build is now signed with the same key, so updates install over the old app. **One last time only:** if you installed an earlier Android or TV version, uninstall it first, then install this one (on a TV: Downloader app → nova-izuki.github.io/tv). From now on it updates itself.

## Ask or Auto in the main chat
The Chat tab now has the same **🛡️ Ask / ⚡ Auto** switch as the orb's chat bar: Ask shows what Izuki wants to run and waits for Allow; Auto runs it straight away and still shows each step.
