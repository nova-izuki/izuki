# Izuki 1.0.61

## Characters that never stand still
- Between replies, the 2D characters and your own 3D face shift their weight from foot to foot, glance around, put their hands on their hips, fold their arms, and stretch now and then.
- Faces move like real ones (your Avaturn face):
  - The nose crinkles with a laugh, and dimples show with a smile.
  - Smiles are slightly uneven, the way real ones are.
  - The lips press and pull to one side while thinking.
  - Tiny brow movements keep the face from ever looking like a mask.
- **Full body stands free on your screen** with no round orb. Head, head and shoulders, and half views sit in the orb. You can change either in the style settings.
- Half-body framing leaves room above the head, and a wave now raises the arm.

## Type to find anything
- The "Or type it" bar on the Draw page also finds parts of Izuki as you type (voice, character, TV, shortcuts…), one tap away. Enter still sends what you typed as a command.
- The Draw page's jump bar now has **Voice** and **Character**. Character jumps straight to the character picker in Settings.
