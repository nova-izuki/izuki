# Izuki 1.0.60

## A 2D character for every voice
- All 39 voices now have a character of their own, drawn live as vector art. Dre has waves and a hoodie, Nia has puffs and hoops, Alfred has a bow tie, Captain Salty has a bandana and eye patch, and so on.
- Two styles: **Flat vector** (clean, bold, motion-design) and **Comic**, Spider-Verse style: ink lines, halftone and hatched shadows, a print offset, drawn "on twos".
- They stand right on your screen, with no round orb (you can turn the orb back on).
- Show full body, half, head and shoulders, or just the head.
- "My voice's" character changes with the voice. Picking a character also gives Izuki their voice.
- New installs start with the 2D character. Your 3D faces and orbs are all still there.

## They act out what Izuki says
- An animator reads each reply and plans the gestures: a wave for "hey", fingers counting "first… second…", a hand to the chin for "I think", palms up for a question, a fist pump or cheer for "let's go!", a point for "you", a shrug for "sorry".
- Mouths make the real shapes of the words (ah, ee, oh, oo, m-b-p, f-v, th…), timed to the voice and closing between sentences.
- Faces react: cheeks lift and eyes squint with a smile, brows go up for questions and surprise, eyes glance up while thinking.
- They breathe, shift their weight, blink, glance around and follow your pointer. Tap one and it jumps.

## Your face (Avaturn), acting too
- Real mouth shapes from the words, using your rig's 15 speech shapes, with teeth.
- Smiles reach the cheeks and eyes, brows react, and it nods on the stressed words.
- Arms gesture with the words (wave, point, open hands, hand to chin, cheer), on top of your idle animation.
- In full body it stands right on your screen, without the orb.

## Ask for it
- "Switch to your 2D character", "make your style comic", "flat style", "show your full body", "just your head".
