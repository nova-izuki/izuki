# Izuki 1.0.40

Izuki speaks up by itself, answers PC questions in the background, proves every change it makes — and controls Samsung and LG TVs too.

## Buddy mode — it speaks up by itself
Like a friend at your desk, Izuki now brings things up without being asked: an important email (a job, an interview, money, a deadline — the AI decides what matters, not every newsletter), a meeting coming up, the battery getting low, the drive nearly full, the internet dropping and coming back, and a nudge after hours without a break. Come back after a while away and it says "welcome back" with what you missed. It never talks over a film or game unless it's urgent, stays quiet at night, and while you're away the important things go to your phone (Telegram / Discord). Small, undoable things it simply handles and tells you — battery saver switches on when the battery's nearly empty and off again when you plug in. Switch any of it off under Talk to Izuki.

## Answers in the background
Ask the orb "how many GB is Zoom?", "which apps don't I use?" or "how much space is left?" and Izuki checks quietly behind the scenes and just tells you — no Settings or File Explorer windows opening. Say "while I watch" or "on my screen" to see it done on screen, or "in the background" to keep it off-screen.

## Proof check
After every change Izuki makes — uninstalling, installing, deleting, closing an app — it looks again and shows "✓ Checked: Zoom is no longer installed" (or "⚠ still there") before it says it's done. And it catches itself: a reply that claims something was done when nothing ran, quotes sizes it never measured, or uses a command that doesn't exist is sent back to be corrected before you see it.

## One clean reply in the chat
A job is now one message, the way Claude Code shows it: the steps it took, then the answer — no more "I'll take care of it" followed by a second "done" message. The status line says what it's really doing ("▶ Uninstalling Zoom…", "📏 Measuring…") instead of sitting on "Thinking…". Quick, harmless checks run without an Allow button. Replies show tidy lists, bold text, clickable links and code boxes with a Copy button, and every reply has Copy, Read aloud and Again. Auto-run mode now really runs commands (it used to refuse them).

## Samsung and LG TVs
"Open Netflix on the TV", "turn the TV up", "pause the TV", "type stranger things on the TV" — now with Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) TVs as well as Roku, over your Wi-Fi with nothing to install. The first time, press Allow on the TV. And a TV request always goes to the TV, even mid-conversation.
