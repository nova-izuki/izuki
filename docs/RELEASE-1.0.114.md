# Izuki 1.0.114: a ⋯ menu on replies, and an offline mode

## ⋯ More, under every reply (free)
The latest reply in the Chat tab, and in the phone app, now has **Copy**, **Read aloud** and a **⋯** menu:
- **Make it shorter**, **Explain it simpler**, **Tell me more**
- **Translate…** (type any language)
- **Save as a note** (PC)
- **Share** (or copy, where sharing isn't available)

It works with whichever AI you use, and with the free ones too.

## 📴 Offline mode
When the PC has no internet and no AI on it (like Ollama), Izuki still helps:
- **Still working offline:** opening apps and folders, volume and music keys, scrolling and tabs, "click Subscribe"-style clicks on named buttons, reminders and timers, Air control, the Windows tweaks, Izuki's voice and hearing.
- **New offline answers:** the time and date, and maths ("15% of 80", "(3 + 4) × 2", "12 times 4").
- **"What works offline?"** gets you the list.
- **Anything that needs the internet is saved to your Missions.** When the internet comes back, Izuki tells you and does those missions by itself.
- **Things that are final or hard to undo are never saved to run later.** That covers sending, emailing, posting, deleting, paying, buying, cancelling and signing out. Izuki asks you to say it again once you're back online, so you can confirm it then.
- **Greetings and "thanks" aren't saved**, and the same request is only ever saved once.

For full answers with no internet at all, add a local AI (Ollama) in Settings.
