# Izuki 1.0.91: characters really animated, and Air control everywhere

## Real animation
21 characters now move with real frame-by-frame animation, every pose redrawn from the character's own painting. The other 14 are on the way and use the bending method until then.
- **Walking:** a real side-on walk cycle, with clothes and hair swinging (the agbada flows). They walk over to what Izuki is about to click and point at it.
- **Talking:** real hand gestures, and the mouth moves while Izuki speaks.
- **Thinking and working:** a hand on the chin; typing on a small laptop while Izuki works.
- **Reactions:** a wave hello, thumbs up, cheering when something's done, a shrug when it isn't.
- **Clean and straight:** they stand straight and are cut out cleanly (no floor shadows under the shoes), and each movement blends smoothly into the next.

## Air control, one tap anywhere
The 🖐️ button turns Air control on or off from the chat, the round chat bubble, the Island, the Chat tab and the tray menu.

## Gone: the always-on desktop character
"Show my mate" is removed. It stayed on the desktop all the time and slowed PCs down. The characters above replace it: they show up when you ask.
