# Izuki 1.0.64

## Free AI, but sharp
- **Every strong free model on your keys.** Free limits are per model, not per account. Once a day Izuki asks each provider you have a key for which models it offers, keeps the strong ones that can read a screenshot, and rotates between them. When one hits its limit, the next carries on, and only that model rests.
  On this PC that means Gemini Flash and Flash-Lite (six models), NVIDIA's Llama 3.2 **90B** Vision (instead of the weak 11B), plus the free OpenRouter and Groq models.
- **Spread across providers:** it takes the best model from each provider in turn, so one provider's per-minute limit never holds up the rest.
- **Tiny models never drive your screen** while a strong one is free. They used to "answer" without doing anything.
- **No more 22-second stalls:** a stuck brain gives up after 14 seconds, and a brain that's been slow gets a backup racing it, even in economy mode.
- **Faster screen reading:** apps that draw their own screens, like FL Studio, could take Windows 15+ seconds to describe. Izuki now waits at most 2.5 seconds, then goes by the picture.

## Beats straight into FL Studio
- "Make beats for me in FL Studio" builds a real drum groove and opens it in FL Studio as a new project. No genre is needed; say "afrobeats", "drill", "amapiano"… for a particular style. It's saved in Music › Izuki Beats too.
