# Izuki 1.0.88: Air control — your hand drives the PC

## Control your PC with your hand in the air
Turn on **Settings → Air control** (or say "turn on air control") and use your hand in front of any camera: built-in, USB or virtual. It picks the right camera itself, and you can choose one.

| Move | What it does |
|---|---|
| ✋ Move your hand | moves the pointer (smooth when still, quick when you move) |
| 🤏 Thumb + index | click; hold and move to drag and drop |
| 🤌 Thumb + middle | right-click |
| 💍 Thumb + ring | copy |
| 🤙 Thumb + pinky | paste |
| ✌️ Two fingers up | scroll by moving up and down |
| 🖐️ Open palm, quick swipe | back / forward |
| 👉 Point at a text box and hold still | clicks it, then listens: what you say is typed in. Ask for something ("reply that I'll be there at 6") and it's written for you, but never sent |
| ✊ Fist, held | pause / resume |

- **A box hugs whatever is under your hand,** sized to it: a whole button or one tiny FL Studio step. A pinch lands right in the middle, so small targets are easy to hit.
- **Your own gestures:** teach it a hand pose and give it keys to press, words to type or a request for Izuki. Hold the pose for a moment to use it.
- **Easy on the battery:** small camera frames on the graphics chip, full speed only while it sees a hand, and it switches itself off after 10 minutes without one.
