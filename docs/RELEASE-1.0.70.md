# Izuki 1.0.70: Ink, a painted character style

## Ink (painted)
A third look for the 2D characters, next to Flat vector and Comic, in the style of painted character concept art:
- Taller figures with smaller heads, standing with their weight on one leg.
- Muted colour blocks with one loud accent colour.
- Hard, brushy shadow planes instead of outlines, and a dry-brush grain.
- Angular faces: a jaw corner, a shadowed cheekbone and nose plane, small dark eyes.
- Boxy, oversized tops, baggy trousers that stack at the hem, and chunky soled boots.
- Off-white paper behind them, with a soft shadow under the feet.

Pick it under Settings → Voice orb → Character style, on the phone app's character settings, or say "make your style painted" (or "ink").

## Fixes
- In Comic style, Atlas (the robot) could fail to draw on its very first frame. It now draws from the start.
