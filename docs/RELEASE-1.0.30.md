# Izuki 1.0.30

Real water, a ferrofluid orb that dances to your music, and a start-up fix.

## Fixed

- **Nothing showed at start-up until the hand was switched off and on.**
  Izuki told its screen layer to appear before that layer had finished
  loading, so the message was lost. Now the layer asks what to show as soon
  as it's ready — the orb, the Island and the listening waveform (for the
  Ctrl + Windows + Space talk key) all work right away.

## Water that looks like water

- **Clear water** is colourless now — no blue tint — and see-through in the
  middle like a real drop. When it hears loud sound, sharp liquid spikes
  rise around it and pulse with the beat.

## Ferrofluid

- New orb: **Ferrofluid** — glossy black magnetic liquid that reflects the
  room and grows sharp spikes with every beat and word. (Or say "change your
  orb to ferrofluid".)

## Music mode

- When music plays, open the Island and tap **✨ Flow with it** — the orb
  comes up and moves with the music. Or turn on **Talk → Dance with my
  music** and it happens by itself whenever music plays. Close it with ×
  (it stays closed until that music stops).
- Only your PC's volume level is read — nothing is recorded.
- **About this song** on the Island's Now Playing card: Izuki tells you
  what the song's about and something interesting about it.
