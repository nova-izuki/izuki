# Izuki 1.0.174: it offers to write down your call before you've thought of it

## 🎧 "Want me to write this down?"
Join a Zoom call, a Teams meeting, a Google Meet or an online class, and Izuki asks:

> *Looks like you're in Weekly Standup. Want me to write it down and turn it into notes? You won't have to follow it.*

Say yes and that's the whole job done. It records what your PC plays (so headphones are fine), writes it down as it goes, and when the call ends you get structured notes — topics, definitions, what was stressed, open questions — each with its time, and the full transcript underneath so anything can be checked.

**Why it asks rather than waiting to be asked:** nobody remembers to start a recorder in the ten seconds before a lecture begins, which is exactly when it matters.

- Only when a real meeting window comes to the front, never the Zoom home screen or a tab left open.
- **Once per meeting**, never while already recording, and it follows the same quiet hours and don't-interrupt rules as everything else.
- No audio is ever kept — each piece is written down and the sound dropped.

## 🖐️ Air control, checked
Air control (your hand on camera driving the pointer) was gone through looking for the one failure that would really hurt: a pinch leaving the mouse button **physically held down** after the camera is unplugged, Air is switched off, or the overlay closes mid-drag — which would leave your PC selecting and dragging everything.

It can't happen. The code tells apart a pinch that actually pressed a button from one still deciding whether it's a 3D grip, and only releases what was really pressed. No change needed — now written down so nobody has to work it out again.
