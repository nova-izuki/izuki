# Izuki 1.0.44

The original hologram face is back, and website tasks stop getting stuck on pop-ups.

## The hologram face is back
By popular demand, the Hologram face is the realistic one again — on the PC and the phone.

## Smoother on websites
- Pop-ups (cookie banners, "sign in" boxes, "allow notifications") are closed once — waiting a moment if one is still sliding in — and then ignored, instead of eating a whole task.
- When a brain says a request is "too large" (Groq's free tier), it's no longer shut out for a minute — it's still used for everything smaller, so there's always a fast brain to fall back on.
- The browser extension reconnects by itself if Izuki restarts after an update (it used to stay off for the whole session).

## Writes like a person
Anything Izuki writes for you — messages, posts, notes, journals — now comes out in plain everyday words and short sentences, unless you ask for formal writing.
