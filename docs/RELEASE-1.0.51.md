# Izuki 1.0.51

More natural 3D faces with far more detail around the eyes, nose and lips; tap the face and it reacts; and a TV panel on the status screen with "What's on my TV?".

## Better-looking 3D faces
- About three times more detail where it counts: the eyes, nose and lips.
- Almond-shaped eyes with curved lids and a slight lift at the outer corner.
- A refined nose, more natural lips and smoother cheeks, brows and temples.
- Finer stubble, and a cleaner hairline.
- The TV's face frames are redrawn to match.

## Tap the face
- Tap the 3D avatar or hologram bust and it reacts: a little jolt, a blink and a grin.

## Your TV on the status screen
- *"Wake up"* now shows **ON YOUR TV** (Roku): what's open right now, with play/pause, volume, home and **What should I watch?**
- Ask *"What's on my TV?"* any time, from the PC, the phone or Telegram.
