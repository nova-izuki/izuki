# Izuki 1.0.86: your character on your desktop, every character painted, FL Studio, and a voice that never drops

## Your desktop mate
Your character now lives on your desktop, with a pet. Settings → **Desktop mate** (on by default):
- **It stands on your taskbar** with its cat or puppy on a lead, and wanders along now and then.
- **It dances** when music plays and **dozes off** (zzz) when you step away. It hops and says welcome back when you return.
- **It notices what you do** and reacts in its own voice: switching to your code editor, FL Studio or YouTube, a build finishing ("4 minutes, finally ✅"), a download landing. You'll also get the odd idle thought. You choose how chatty it is: Quiet, Normal or Chatty.
- **Double-click it to talk.** Izuki's whole brain answers, with every tool, screen control and your apps, and the reply shows in its speech bubble. It remembers your chats on this PC; right-click → **Clear memory** wipes them.
- **Drag it anywhere.** Clicks pass straight through everything but the character, so it never gets in your way.
- **It hides itself** during fullscreen games and videos.
- **Easy on the battery:** it only animates quickly while it's moving or talking.

## Every character, painted
All 35 characters, from Nova, Leo, Ada and Sophie to Rex, Alfred and Atlas, now come in the **Concept** style: tall, flat gouache painting, one bold accent colour. Every one talks with its painted mouth and blinks. That's in Settings → Voice orb → Character style → Concept, on the PC and the phone.

## FL Studio co-producer, step one
Settings → **FL Studio** → Set up (one time). Izuki installs its script into FL and makes a private virtual MIDI cable using Windows' own MIDI system, or loopMIDI if FL can't see that one. Then:
- **"Make afrobeats"**, "make a trap beat at 140", "make amapiano": a fresh pattern, with the drums written step by step while you watch, tempo set, playing.
- **"Stop", "play", "tempo 128", "faster"**.

It works through FL's own scripting, not the mouse, so it's exact and fast. Every change is one undo step, and Izuki reads the drums back from FL before saying it's done. Coming next: melodies and 808s in the piano roll, finishing your beat, mixing vocals and fixing timing.

## A voice that never drops
If your chosen voice can't speak (Groq's daily limit, no credits), Izuki now asks **every other voice service you have set up at once**, and the first one to answer speaks. It always keeps the same gender: a woman's voice never turns into a man's mid-conversation. That's true down to the free natural voice and the on-device one too.
