# Izuki 1.0.119: Island 2.0, first part

## ✨ The Nova look
A new Island look: deep dark glass with a cyan-to-violet holographic edge. It **brightens while Izuki is really listening, thinking, working or doing your missions**, and stays calm otherwise. It's a change of style, not an animation, so it costs nothing while it sits over videos all day. In high-contrast mode the edge is always bright. Choose it in Settings → Talk → Island look → ✨ Nova.

## Three tabs when the Island opens
- **Now:** everything the Island already did (music, Ask, camera, reminders, live activities, suggestions, quick actions). A question you're asking stays alive while you switch tabs.
- **Vitals:** a mini JARVIS readout, every number measured from your PC:
  - Processor and memory load, with a plain hint like "Plenty to spare" or "Nearly full".
  - Battery and charging, and whether you're online.
  - Izuki's own memory.
  - What Izuki is working on, or your running mission.

  These are read every 3 seconds, and only while the tab is open.
- **Apps:** open any app by typing its name, your ★ favourites, and what you've used most today. Websites like YouTube work too.

## Size and place
Settings → Talk → **Island size and place**: Small, Normal or Large, and Left, Top centre or Right with one tap. You can still drag it. The large size stays fully on screen.
