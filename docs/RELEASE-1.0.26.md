# Izuki 1.0.26

Real-looking orbs, a status report on "wake up", and clicks that land.

## Orbs that look real

- **Clear water, Tidal pearl and Star crystal are now drawn in 3D** on your
  graphics card, with real light: the water drop shows the world
  upside-down inside it with a rainbow edge and light gathered at the
  bottom; the pearl shimmers as the angle changes; the crystal holds a
  nebula and stars that twinkle when Izuki talks. All three ripple with
  your voice and Izuki's. Same look on the phone.
- Older PCs without 3D graphics keep the previous look automatically.

## "Hey Nova, wake up"

- Say **"wake up"**, **"status report"**, **"how are the apps doing"** or
  **"catch me up"** and Izuki reports in a second — no AI needed: the time,
  your battery, how your PC is doing (memory and free space, with a heads-up
  if either is low), today's reminders, what's playing and which apps are
  linked. Then: "What are we doing first?"

## Atlas

- A new companion: **Atlas**, a calm, quietly witty British AI assistant —
  the film-AI feel, on a free neural voice. On the PC and the phone.

## Clicks land where you meant

- **Circle a button and say "next"** (or "click next", "submit", "click
  it"): Izuki clicks that exact button at once, with no AI to wait for — so
  a busy free AI can't slow it down or get it wrong. If the button's name
  doesn't match what you said, it asks the AI instead.
- When you've drawn on the screen, **the AI can no longer click the
  taskbar** by mistake (it once clicked Windows Search instead of the
  circled "Next"). A stray taskbar click is moved onto the button you
  circled, or not made.

## Conversations carry on

- After "open a YouTube video", saying or typing **"continue"**, **"play the
  next one"** or **"now pause it"** carries on with that task instead of
  starting from nothing — in the orb's chat bar, the Chat tab and the phone.
- Quick commands ("open YouTube", "louder") are part of the conversation
  now too.

## Keep the answer up

- New: **Talk → Keep my last answer on screen**. Izuki's answer stays after
  the orb goes, with a Copy button, until you close it.
