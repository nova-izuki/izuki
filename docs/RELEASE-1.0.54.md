# Izuki 1.0.54

Real 3D faces, your own face that talks, and updates that go straight to the newest version.

## Real 3D faces
- Four new faces under **Voice orb**, made as real 3D models: **Hologram woman**, **Hologram man**, **Woman** and **Man**.
- They breathe, sway, turn to look at your mouse and talk with the voice: the jaw opens and the lips part. Tap one and it reacts.
- **Customise** each face: skin tone, glow colour, hologram glow, shine, size, height and turn.
- They replace the older drawn "3D avatar" and "Hologram bust". If you were using one, you're moved to the nearest new face. The original hologram face and all the orbs are still there.

## Your own face
- **My face**: make a 3D you on avaturn.me (free, a few minutes), then pick the .glb file. It has a real face rig: your mouth moves with Izuki's voice, your eyes blink and look around, and your body idles. Choose **Head only** if you like.
- Your face is kept only on your device. It's never uploaded.

## Add any face
- **Add a face**: any .glb (Tencent Hunyuan3D, Meshy, Sketchfab…), as many as you like. Floating bits that generated models often come with are cleaned off automatically.

## Ask for it
- "Switch to the hologram woman", "use my face", "make your glow pink", "just your head", "make your face bigger".

## Everywhere
- The PC, the phone app and the TV page all have the same picker, customising and uploads.
- The Roku channel shows the new faces (channel build 54; it updates itself).

## Updates straight to the newest
- An old copy of Izuki (an old installer, or a PC that's been off for a while) now goes straight to the newest version, never through the ones in between.
- Right after Izuki starts, a new version downloads and installs itself (if automatic checks are on and you're not in the middle of something). Later in the day it's ready for one tap.
- Just before installing, Izuki checks once more. If an even newer version has come out, it installs that one instead.
