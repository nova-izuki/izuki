# Izuki 1.0.223: faster, an ask bar, and a microphone that hears you

## ⚡ On your PC

- **Alt+Space, anywhere: the ask bar.** One glowing box opens near the top of the screen. Type, press Enter, and the answer shows right under your question; ask it to do something and Izuki does it. Three ideas to try are waiting when it opens. Esc or a click outside closes it. (Change the key in Settings → Shortcuts.)
- **Searches are instant.** "Find a pizza place near me", "search for cheap flights", "look up the weather in Lagos", "google how tall is Everest" open the results at once, with no AI to wait for. One of these used to take over a minute.
- **Web pages are read, not photographed.** When Izuki works in your browser, it reads the page's own buttons and text instead of sending a screenshot every step: faster answers, and the free AI allowance lasts much longer. It still takes a screenshot when you ask about how something looks, or when a step doesn't work.
- **Busy free AIs are left alone.** When OpenRouter's free models are swamped, Izuki rests all of them for a minute instead of trying the next one and the next (that failed over 200 times in one morning).
- **A muted microphone is caught.** If your mic only sends silence because Windows has it muted or turned right down, Izuki tells you, and "unmute my mic" turns it back on.
- Changing the "hold to dictate" shortcut works straight away, without restarting Izuki.

## 🥽 Izuki in VR

Already live at izukinova.ai/vr and in the IZUKI NOVA app for Meta Quest:

- **Physics you can watch.** "Simulate a pendulum", "throw a ball at 60 degrees", "drop a feather and a hammer on the Moon", "how do satellites orbit", a mass on a spring, a sound wave: real equations at real speed, with the formula and live numbers. Change it while it runs: "now on the Moon", "make it longer", "at 30 degrees", "faster", "again".
- **Handle a hologram.** Grab any part and move your hand sideways to turn it (let go mid-swing and it keeps spinning). Or say "spin it", "turn it around", "make it bigger", "explode it" (the parts fly apart so you can see inside) and "put it back together".
- **Real planets and the live space station**, and **real photos** beside holograms.
- **"Play … on YouTube"** opens YouTube beside you (the 3D room can't show videos, so Izuki steps out while it plays).
- **A headset isn't a phone:** phone-only settings (Drive mode, iPhone alarms, installing to a phone) are hidden in the headset, and the app's words say "headset".
