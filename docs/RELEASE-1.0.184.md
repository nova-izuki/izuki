# Izuki 1.0.184: counting brains the way people count them

## 🔢 A one-line correction to 1.0.183

The new self-diagnosis counted *models*, not AIs. One free key offers six
models, so the honest sentence was **"2 of my 18 brains are benched"** — true of
models, meaningless to a person — and it made the most useful line in the whole
answer unreachable: *"all of them are out, so asking again this second can't
help."* Rests are per AI, so the count is now per AI too: **"2 of my 5 AI brains
are benched right now: Groq 14s, Gemini 1m 40s."**

Everything else in 1.0.183 — the characters standing straight, the 20-second
screen looks, the second round when every brain times out — is unchanged.
