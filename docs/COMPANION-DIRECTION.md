# Companion direction and honest capability boundaries

Reference research: [tinyPeople / tinyNature](https://natura.inc/tinypeople), [tinyNature](https://natura.inc/tinynature), [Volta's fluid headphones](https://checkout.volta.audio/).

TinyNature's public pages emphasize remembered context, proactive day briefings and continuity across messaging channels. Some specialist agents are explicitly marked coming soon. These are product claims, not integrations we have verified or inherited.

Izuki's direction is a companion that explains what it knows, remembers what you choose, and performs verifiable actions. An app catalog entry isn't proof of account access; a generated plan isn't proof of a completed action; a QR scan isn't proof of a working connection.
