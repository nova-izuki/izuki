Izuki browser extension privacy

Updated October 30, 2026.

What the extension uses

The extension can read page text, page addresses, selections and tab information to answer questions about pages you choose, perform browser tasks you request, and offer its optional focus features. Connecting an AI tab lets it place a question in that tab. Optional Follow questions reads the chosen page while it is enabled.

Where content goes

When you use Izuki on your PC, page content and your prompt go to the local Izuki desktop app. The desktop app can send that content to the AI provider configured there. When you choose another AI website, the question and shared page content go to that website. Those providers have their own privacy terms. Enabling extra-tab context includes the tabs you choose to share.

What stays in the browser

The extension uses browser storage for preferences, connection information and conversation history. You can clear history in the panel. Removing the extension removes its browser storage according to your browser's controls.

Your choices

You can turn page sharing and Follow questions off, change or disconnect the AI tab, stop an active request, and remove the extension. Desktop actions use the desktop app's task controls. Installing the extension does not give a remote website permission to control your PC.

Developer services

The extension has no advertising or analytics service of its own. Content you send to an AI provider is processed by that provider. To report a privacy issue, use the project issue tracker; avoid including private page content or account credentials.
