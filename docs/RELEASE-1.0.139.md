# Izuki 1.0.139: APA 7 references from the real source

For your labs and papers: **"cite this page in APA"**, **"APA reference for 10.1037/…"** (a DOI) or **"cite ISBN 978…"**.

- **The details come from the source itself, never made up:**
  - a DOI from Crossref, the official registry for journal articles;
  - an ISBN from Open Library;
  - a web page from its own author, date and title tags. If the page names a DOI, Crossref's full record is used instead.
- **It's put together the APA 7 way:**
  - authors as "Mensah, A. K., & Osei, B.";
  - the date, or "(n.d.)" when none was published;
  - titles in sentence case, with the journal, volume, issue and pages;
  - the DOI as a link;
  - *asterisks* marking what goes in italics.
- **You get the in-text citation too:** (Mensah & Osei, 2023), or (Mensah et al., 2023) for three or more authors.
- **Every reference is saved** to today's "References (APA)" note.
- **It tells you what to check**, like capitalising names inside a title, or a source with no date or author.
- It works from your phone too: send it a link, DOI or ISBN.

For labs and assignments, Izuki also explains each question and the theory behind it, helps you outline your answer, and checks your draft against the instructions. The answers themselves stay yours to write.
