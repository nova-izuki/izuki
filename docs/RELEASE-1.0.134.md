# Izuki 1.0.134: your cursor stays on the Island, quiet hours, and a Study log

## 🖱️ The cursor no longer hides on the Island
Moving the mouse over the open Island, the chat bubble, the camera window or the Air keyboard used to make the pointer vanish. Izuki's overlay hid the Windows cursor everywhere, and these are the places where the overlay takes the mouse. Now Izuki's own clickable things show the normal cursor, a hand on buttons and a text cursor in typing boxes.

## 📝 Study log
Say "save every question and answer to my notes", or switch on Notes → Study log.
- From then on, each question you ask goes into today's **Study log** note, one note a day. That covers typed questions, spoken ones, the phone and deep research.
- Answers are written to teach: the answer first, then why or how, then one line starting "Remember:".
- Commands ("open Chrome") and small talk aren't saved.
- In Notes, any Study log can become flashcards or a quiz.
- "Stop saving my questions" turns it off. What's saved stays.

## 🌙 Quiet hours
Set them in Apps → Heads-ups → Quiet hours, or in Draw → Hands-free.
- Heads-ups (email, meetings, school, missions) wait and come as **one summary** when quiet hours end.
- The Island stops popping up suggestions. "Wake up" in its right-click menu brings it back until quiet hours end.
- Timers and alarms you set still ring.
- Missions heads-ups now have their own switch.

## 🏝️ More on the Island
- **Quick note:** open the Island, type in the 📝 box, press Enter. It's saved to Notes.
- **What's due:** "3 things due this week, next: …" from your school calendar; tap it for the full list. Anything due within a day shows on the pill.
- **Website down:** if a business website in Empire stops answering, the Island says so, with Check again. Sites are looked at every 3 hours while up, every 25 minutes while down.
