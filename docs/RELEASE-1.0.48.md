# Izuki 1.0.48

The browser extension becomes a smart helper of its own — not just Izuki's eyes for the orb.

## Izuki in your browser (extension 1.1)
- **Right-click anything.** Select text on any page, right-click → **Izuki**: *Explain this*, *Translate to English*, *Sum it up*, *Read it aloud*, *Save to Nova Notes* or *Remind me later*. Right-click a link: *Sum up this link* — without opening it. Right-click the page: *Sum up this page*, *Save this page to Nova Notes* (as study notes) or *Read this page aloud*.
- **Answers right on the page**, in a neat bubble with Copy and Read aloud.
- **Ask about any page.** Press **Alt+Shift+I**, or click the Izuki button in the toolbar and type a question — plus one-tap *Sum up this page*, *Read it aloud*, *What should I do?* and *Save to Notes*.
- **Read aloud works even when Izuki isn't open** — it uses the browser's own voice.
- **Focus guard.** During Izuki's Focus mode, opening YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Netflix, Reddit and the like shows a calm "You're focusing — 14 min left" screen: *Back to work* or *Just 5 minutes*.
- Answers come from Izuki on your PC with your own AI — the extension holds no keys and sends nothing anywhere else.

**Already have the extension?** Download it again (Settings → Browser extension → download), unzip it over the old folder, and press ↻ reload on Izuki in chrome://extensions or edge://extensions.
