# Izuki 1.0.56

On Android TVs (Google TV, Fire TV, Android TV) with the Izuki app, Izuki can now see the screen.

## "What's on my screen?"
- While you're watching, ask "Hey Nova, what's on my screen?", "what does it say?" or "is this free?". Izuki takes a picture of the screen and tells you (Android 11 and newer).
- Apps with copy protection (Netflix's video, for example) hide their picture. Izuki then reads the words on screen instead: menus, sign-in and subscription screens.

## Paywall guard
- When an app opens a "Start free trial", "Choose a plan" or "$9.99/month" screen, a note pops up over it: "Netflix wants a subscription here. Free on this TV: Tubi, Pluto TV — say 'Hey Nova, open Tubi'."
- It checks the words on screen right on the TV. Nothing is kept or sent anywhere, and each app gets at most one note every 10 minutes.
- It's on once "Control this TV" is on. Switch it off with the new **Paywall guard** tile.

Roku TVs don't allow any app to see the screen, so this works on Android-based TVs only.
