# Izuki 1.0.133: Empire, part one — your business, a real website check, and "what needs my attention?"

The Empire spec asks for real software, one real platform at a time, not a mockup. With no business platform to connect yet, this first part is the general version: everything here works today, and shops and payments plug in later.

## 🏢 Your business (Apps → Empire)
Add a business: its name, what it does, its website, its hours and its goals. Several businesses are kept separately, so their information never mixes. Remove one with the bin icon; nothing on the website changes.

## 🌐 "Check my website"
A real check, not a guess:
- **Up or down**, how fast the home page loads, and whether it's **secure (HTTPS)**.
- **Broken links:** up to 25 of the home page's own links are tried, 8 at a time.
- **What Google and phones see:**
  - a missing or too-long title or description;
  - no main heading;
  - images without descriptions;
  - no mobile viewport setting;
  - no sitemap.xml or robots.txt;
  - a slow (over 3 s) or very heavy page.
- **Each problem says what to do about it.**
- **Compared with the last check**, so "It was up at the last check" or "slower than last time (0.9 s)" stands out. The last 20 checks are kept.
- **Any site:** "is example.com down?"

Tested live on example.com: up, 0.4 s, HTTPS, and four real things to fix. I confirmed in the page itself that it has no heading, sitemap or robots.txt.

## ✋ "What needs my attention?"
One briefing from what's actually connected:
- Approvals waiting for your OK.
- Important and unread email from the last day.
- Today's reminders.
- A running mission.
- Accounts that need signing in again.
- Each business's website: re-checked if the last check is over 6 hours old.

It **always ends with what it checked and what it couldn't**, so a missing piece is never mistaken for "nothing there".

**Morning briefing:** say "every weekday at 8, give me my business briefing" (Routines).

Read only: Empire doesn't buy, send, post or change anything.
