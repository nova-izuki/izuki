# Izuki 1.0.144: Izuki keeps working in its own tab

## 📌 Open another tab: Izuki keeps going in its own
When Izuki starts a task in Chrome or Edge, it now **pins that tab**. Switch to another tab to read, watch or work on something else, and Izuki **carries on in its own tab**: it reads the page, clicks, types and scrolls there through the browser extension. It never touches your mouse or keyboard, and never the tab you're on.
- The Island and status say **"Working in its own tab — use your other tabs freely."**
- A step that really needs the screen (a keyboard shortcut, a right-click, dragging) **waits until you go back to that tab yourself**. Izuki never pulls you over to it.
- While you're on another tab, Izuki doesn't draw on your screen or move the hand, since that would land on your tab.
- Close Izuki's tab and it simply carries on with whatever's in front.
- Every safety rule still applies in its own tab: nothing is sent, bought or deleted without your yes, and quiz and test answers are never clicked.

**Needs the browser extension 1.4.2:** Settings → Browser extension shows "update it" until you have it. Download it again, unzip it over the old folder, and press reload in chrome://extensions.
