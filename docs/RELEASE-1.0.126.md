# Izuki 1.0.126: the Style tab, with 14 looks, Auto, and 51 tweaks

You asked for more Windhawk-style tweaks, a complete new layout instead of a long list, things that change by themselves, and animation. Looks and tweaks now live in their own **Style** tab (next to Settings).

## A live preview of your desktop
The top of the tab is a big picture of your desktop: the wallpaper, a window with your accent-coloured title bar, a clock widget and the taskbar.
- **Hover any look** and the preview fades to it, with its colours on the window and taskbar.
- **Tap it** and a sweep of light runs across as it goes on.
- Stars twinkle while your mouse is over the preview.
- Nothing moves when you're not looking or pointing at it, so Izuki stays light on your PC.

Below are three sections side by side: **Looks · Tweaks · Auto**, laid out in tiles so there's little scrolling.

## 🎨 7 new looks (14 in all)
Each one has a wallpaper Izuki paints for your exact screen:
- **Synthwave:** a striped 80s sun over a glowing neon grid.
- **Sunset:** the sun going down behind purple hills.
- **Ocean:** moonlight on rolling night waves.
- **Matrix:** green code raining down a black screen.
- **Arctic:** bright icy mountains under a pale sun.
- **Galaxy:** a pink and blue nebula full of stars.
- **Dune:** warm sand dunes in the afternoon sun.

Wallpapers now change through Windows' own wallpaper interface, which **fades smoothly** from the old picture to the new one. The older direct method is still there as a fallback.

## 🔁 Auto: looks that change by themselves
- **Day & night:** pick a look for the day and one for the night, and the times (07:00 and 19:00 to start with). Times that cross midnight work too.
- **Shuffle:** a new look every 30 minutes, hour, 3 hours or day, going through the looks you choose (or all of them).
- Choose whether the wallpaper changes too.
- Izuki checks once a minute and only changes when it's time.
- **Undo** turns Auto off, so it can't bring a look straight back.

## 🛠️ 51 Windows tweaks, as tiles, in 7 groups
All of them use only your own Windows settings (no admin, nothing injected into Windows), and every one flips back.
- **Taskbar:** app names on the taskbar (never combine), hide the Widgets button, and more.
- **Start:** hide recent files, hide most-used apps, no Microsoft account reminders.
- **Desktop:** "This PC", your folder, hide the Recycle Bin and "Learn about this picture" icons, a clean desktop with no icons, and full-quality wallpaper (no compression).
- **File Explorer:**
  - Open to This PC, Home without recent files.
  - The full path in the title, tick boxes to select files.
  - No "- Shortcut" on new shortcuts.
- **Look & cursor:** Windows' own black, big black, big white and inverted pointer sets.
- **Speed & gaming:** snappy windows (no animations), faster menus, no startup delay, no background game recording, no Sticky Keys pop-up.
- **Quiet & private:** no tips while you work, no "what's new" screens, no "finish setting up" nags, no advertising ID, no tailored experiences.

**One-tap packs:** Gamer, Clean & private, and Power user. Each group shows how many tweaks are on.

I tested writing every existing tweak value back unchanged on this PC, and Windows accepted all of them. The Widgets button is the one Windows protects on some versions; if it refuses, Izuki says so.
