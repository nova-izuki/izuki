# Izuki 1.0.73: your face moves like a person

For **My Face** and any full-body 3D character with a rig:

- **Never through the body.** After every pose, Izuki checks whether the hand or forearm ended up inside the torso and, if so, brings the arm forward until it's clear.
- **Human gestures.** Talking happens in front of the waist, not with arms flung wide. A shrug keeps the elbows in, palms up and shoulders lifted. "Thinking" is one hand at the chin with the other across the waist, so the hands no longer meet in the middle. Asking or explaining turns the palms up.
- **Fingers that move.** A loose natural curl at rest, a real pointing finger, a fist for "let's go", and counting on fingers.
- **Never frozen.** With no idle animation of its own, a resting arm hangs a touch forward with a soft elbow and sways with the breath.
