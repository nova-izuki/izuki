# Izuki 1.0.34

"Wake up" now reads your inbox and calendar, and the TV always gets TV requests.

## Wake up, with your inbox

- The status screen and briefing now tell you how many new emails came
  today and who the latest are from (an **INBOX** panel), and what's left on
  your Google Calendar today (in **TODAY**, with your reminders) — when those
  apps are linked. All read at once, so it stays quick.

## Fixed

- **TV requests went to the PC.** Only some phrasings ("on the TV") were
  sent to the TV. Now any request that mentions the TV goes to the TV, and it
  understands more: "TV, Netflix", "play Stranger Things on the TV",
  "watch YouTube on TV", and "control my TV" (checks it's connected).
- **✕ / Cancel stopped working in draw mode** (after tapping Draw on the
  Island). The drawing tools now always take clicks, and a finished stroke
  can never leave them stuck.

## Smarter suggestions

- **Chat tab:** the ideas fit the moment — what's on your screen (a quiz,
  an email, an error, a video) plus the time of day and the weekend —
  instead of the same four every time.
- **Apps:** the example requests ("What's in my inbox today?") are buttons
  now — tap one to ask it.

## Jarvis voice

- **Atlas** is labelled as the **Jarvis-style AI assistant** voice in
  Characters, and saying "Jarvis voice", "be Jarvis" or "switch to Jarvis"
  turns it on. ("Jarvis mode" stays the no-mouse control style.)
