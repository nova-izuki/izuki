# Izuki 1.0.47

Izuki reads to you, types what you say anywhere, knows your screen time and shows the weather — on the PC, the phone and the TV.

## Read it to me
Select text anywhere — an article, an email, a PDF — and say "Hey Nova, read this to me". With nothing selected, it reads the page in front of you. Long reads come in parts: "keep reading" carries on. Your clipboard is left exactly as it was.

## Talk to type, anywhere
"Hey Nova, type what I say" — click into Word, WhatsApp, Google Docs or any text box and just talk: your words are typed where the cursor is, with "comma", "full stop", "question mark", "new line" and "new paragraph" understood and capitals tidied. "Stop typing" ends it.

## Screen time
"How long was I on YouTube today?", "what did I spend most time on?", "screen time yesterday". Izuki counts minutes per app and big site (YouTube, Netflix, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp…) — only names and minutes, never what was on screen, on this PC for two weeks — and shows today's top three on the status screen. Switch it off under Talk to Izuki; works from the phone and Telegram too.

## The weather, everywhere
A weather glance on the Island, the status screen, the phone's home screen and the TV's top bar — for the town you set (Talk to Izuki → Your town or city) or the one Izuki remembers you live in. Linked phones and TVs get it from the PC.

## Timers on the TV
"Set a timer for 10 minutes" on the Izuki TV app shows a countdown in the top bar and says when it's done.
