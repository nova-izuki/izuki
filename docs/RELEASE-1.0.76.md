# Izuki 1.0.76: Painted Dre (beta)

A new character style, **Painted (beta)**: a real painting of the character, brought to life.

- **Dre** is the first. He's an original painted character in the concept-art look you asked for: flat painted colour, brushy shadows, one red accent.
- His painted mouth opens with the words, and wider on the open sounds. His eyes blink, and he breathes and sways gently: from the neck in close-ups, from the feet in full body.
- He's cut out cleanly, so he stands right on your desktop with a soft shadow under his boots. Inside the orb he sits on painted paper.
- Pick **Settings → Voice orb → Character style → Painted (beta)**, the phone app's character style, or say "make your style painted". Every other character shows in **Ink** until their own painting is made.
- The character cards wait for the painting to load, so they never show the stand-in.

The art was made with Higgs Field (Seedream 5.0 Flash, 1.5 credits): a base painting plus two matching edits (mouth open, eyes closed), blended in only where the mouth and eyes are.

Reviewed with CodeRabbit (1 finding, fixed).
