# Izuki 1.0.57

## Works beside you
- Use your mouse or keyboard while Izuki is working and it waits for you, then takes a fresh look and carries on. It no longer fights you for the mouse.
- If a click changes nothing, Izuki presses the button directly: through Windows, or inside the web page with the browser extension.
- Before every click, the pointer is checked to be exactly on target. If the control shifts as the pointer arrives, the click follows it.

## PC Boost
- Notices when your PC stays maxed out and offers to speed it up. It clears old temp files and closes hogging background updaters, and names the heavy app.
- It never closes a window you're using. "Fix it by itself" is off unless you switch it on (Settings → PC Boost).
- Or just say "my PC is lagging".

## Alarms and planning
- "Wake me at 6:30 every weekday" sets a real alarm that rings on your PC, with Stop and Snooze.
- On your phone, one tap sets the alarm in your phone's own clock.
- "Plan my day" books the reminders for you.

## Pictures and directions
- Attach, paste or drop pictures in Chat, or send them on Discord or Telegram.
- Paste step-by-step directions from another AI and Izuki carries them out on screen.

## Island, status screen and answers
- When a question is on screen, the Island adds **Answer it for me**: the answer straight up with a one-line why. It never submits for you.
- The status screen has a live processor trace, the heaviest apps, and **Autopilot**: one-tap fixes for low space, battery, a slow PC, a meeting soon or urgent mail.
- "Keep my last answer on screen" now scrolls back through all of today's answers, with a clear button.

## Characters
- Skin tone and hair colour presets.
- Comic and Flat art styles.
- Framing from head to full body, and a no-orb mode.
- The voice and the character switch together to match, or keep your own choice.

## Also
- **Meta Llama** is a new brain choice: paste a free key from llama.developer.meta.com (US). Llama 4 is also a one-tap pick on Groq and NVIDIA. It can see your screen.
- Quieter on battery while waiting for "Hey Nova", and talking over Izuki cuts it off at once.
- A stuck "Thinking…" now recovers by itself and tells you.
- Updates downloaded in the background install within the first 10 minutes after you open Izuki.
- Roku: "what's on my TV" now names the app, the show's progress and the live channel.
