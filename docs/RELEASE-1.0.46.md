# Izuki 1.0.46

The Island comes alive with live activities — timers, downloads, screenshots, charging and recent copies, each with the smart next step — the chat shows its work and runs Auto without asking, and the hologram face gets more lifelike.

## Live activities on the Island
- **Timers.** "Hey Nova, set a pasta timer for 12 minutes", "timer for an hour and a half", "how long's left on the pasta?", "cancel the timer". Each counts down on the Island, several at once, and Izuki tells you out loud when one's done.
- **Downloads.** A download in progress shows on the Island; when it finishes you get Open and Show in folder — plus the right extra for what it is: **Sum it up** for a PDF or document, **Install it** for an installer, **Unzip it** for a zip.
- **Screenshots.** Take one and Izuki reads it right on your PC (no internet): **Copy the text**, and if it shows an error, a question or another language — **Fix this error**, **Explain this question** or **Translate**.
- **Charging.** Plug in or unplug and the Island shows the battery for a moment, like a phone.
- **Recent copies.** Your last few copied texts, one tap to copy again (kept in memory only, never passwords).

## The chat shows its work
- Typing **allow**, **yes** or **go ahead** presses the waiting Allow button (and **no** says no) — instead of the AI replying "I can't click the button for you".
- **⚡ Auto really runs without asking**, even straight after you switch it on.
- Izuki can no longer just say "On it." and do nothing, or claim it's "waiting for your approval" when nothing was started — it's made to actually start the job, and you see each step and the result in the same message.
- The empty Later list no longer sits in the chat — it appears when something's on it.

## Your TV
"Turn off the TV in 30 minutes" — a sleep timer, for Roku, Samsung, LG and the Izuki TV app.

## The hologram face, more lifelike
The realistic Hologram face gets a real face's oval (narrower at the temples, tapering to the chin), a neck, finer detail (brow ridge, nose, lips, cheekbones), a glowing outline and a scan that sweeps down it.
