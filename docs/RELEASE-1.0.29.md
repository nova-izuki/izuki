# Izuki 1.0.29

Your TV joins in, Izuki gets a face, and you can change its look by voice.

## Control your TV

- Say **"open Netflix on the TV"**, **"turn the TV up"**, **"pause the TV"**,
  **"search the TV for Stranger Things"**, **"TV go home"** or **"turn off the
  TV"** — by voice, in chat, from the phone app, Telegram or Discord.
- Works with **Roku** over your Wi-Fi with nothing to install on the TV:
  Izuki finds it by itself (Settings → Control my TV).
- If your Roku is set to only allow limited control, Izuki tells you the one
  setting to change: Settings → System → Advanced system settings → Control
  by mobile apps → Network access → **Default**.
- Other TVs (Android/Google TV, Fire TV, Samsung, LG) are next.

## Hologram face

- **Settings → Voice orb → Hologram face**: a head made of light. Its jaw
  moves with the voice, it blinks, it smiles when Izuki's cheerful and
  softens when it's sympathetic, raises its brows while it thinks, and
  turns its head slowly.

## Change Izuki by voice

- **"Change your orb to stardust"**, **"make your orb pure water"**,
  **"switch to the hologram face"** — done instantly.
- **"Switch to Atlas"**, **"be Kiki"**, **"talk like Alfred"** — Izuki becomes
  that companion and greets you in their voice.

## Status screen boot-up

- The "wake up" status screen now powers on with a quick check-in under the
  clock: voice online, screen linked, apps connected.

## Fixes

- Settings changed by voice (or when the TV is found) now show up at once
  in an open Settings window, and are never overwritten by it.
