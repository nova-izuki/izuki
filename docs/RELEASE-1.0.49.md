# Izuki 1.0.49

Izuki is now patient like a person: it opens each thing once, waits as long as your PC needs, and keeps working in the same browser tab. It also reads your email for the real to-dos, shows every tool it uses in the chat, and understands loose TV requests.

## Patient, like a person
- **Opens each app or page once.** Asked for the same thing again a moment later, Izuki waits for the one that's already coming instead of opening another copy. No more piles of PowerShell windows or duplicate tabs.
- **Waits as long as your PC needs.** Izuki learns how long windows take to appear on your PC. A slow laptop gets more time (up to 25 seconds); a fast one isn't kept waiting. Page loads and screen checks stretch to match.
- **Same tab in the browser.** When Izuki is already working in a browser tab (a search, a page it opened, a new tab), it goes to the next address or search right there: address bar, type, Enter. Your own pages, like a doc or a half-filled form, always keep their tab.

## Tool cards in the chat
- Every command, web search, file read and file save in the Chat tab shows as its own card. Each card has the tool, what it was for, **IN** (exactly what ran) and **OUT** (what came back), with *Show all* for long output.
- A command you approve shows the same card once it runs, with its proof check.

## It really goes through your email
- Ask *"What's important in my email?"* (Gmail connected). Izuki reads the last three days, leaving out promotions and social, and skips the noise. It gives the real to-dos: bills with amounts, people waiting for a reply, deliveries, deadlines, meetings and security alerts.
- The status screen (*"wake up"*) shows **INBOX — WHAT MATTERS**. Tap one and Izuki helps with it. It can draft a reply, but never sends on its own.
- Apps you haven't signed in to are no longer listed as news.

## Ask your TV like a person (Roku)
- Say *"put on something funny for the kids"*, *"a dinosaur cartoon"* or *"what's good tonight?"*. The AI picks a real title from the apps actually installed on your TV.
- When the title is on more than one app, Izuki asks *"Disney Plus or Prime Video?"*. Answer *"Disney"* or *"the first one"*.
- App names are matched loosely: *"fox live"* finds FOX One, *"netflixx"* finds Netflix. *"Sleep in 30 minutes"* and *"play Ice Age on Disney"* work too.

## A fuller Island and status screen
- The Island has one-tap tiles: 5-minute timer, Focus, Read this, Explain my screen, your Later list and Email.
- After a few minutes on the same web page, the Island offers *"Sum up this page?"*.
- The status screen has new one-tap buttons: Go through my email, Focus 25, 5-min timer and Read this page.
