# Izuki 1.0.35

The Izuki channel for Roku, and the ElevenLabs voice explains itself.

## Izuki on your Roku screen

- A real **Izuki channel for Roku**: the orb fills your TV (Stardust — it
  breathes at rest, pulses when Izuki talks, spins while it thinks) with
  Izuki's words underneath like captions, and the time in the corner.
- While it's open, Izuki on your PC follows along there every time you talk
  to it — and never touches the TV when something else is playing.
- Install it free in Roku's developer mode: **Settings → Control my TV →
  Put the Izuki screen on your Roku** walks you through it (download, open
  the Roku's installer, install).

## ElevenLabs

- **"ElevenLabs rejected the key" was wrong.** When the month's free credits
  run out, ElevenLabs answers as if the key were bad. Izuki now says plainly
  that the free credits are used up and will work again when they renew —
  and uses the free voice straight away instead of trying ElevenLabs before
  every sentence.
