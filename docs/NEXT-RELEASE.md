# Waiting for the next release

Codex and Freebuff: add one line per change you pushed. Write it in plain
words for the owner, and put your name in brackets. Claude moves these lines
into the next `docs/RELEASE-x.y.z.md` when it releases, then clears this list.

- **"Any good emails?" means what you meant:** "check my gmail on chrome for any good message", "anything worth reading in my inbox" go through your inbox and pick out what needs you — they used to search for the words "chrome" and "good". Where you'd read it (on Chrome, on my phone) is never taken as the topic; a real topic still searches ("any good emails from Amazon"). [Claude]
- **Heads-ups wait while you're talking:** a heads-up that isn't urgent (like the disk-space one) no longer lands in the middle of your own request or a task. [Claude]
