# Waiting for the next release

Codex and Freebuff: add one line per change you pushed. Write it in plain
words for the owner, and put your name in brackets. Claude moves these lines
into the next `docs/RELEASE-x.y.z.md` when it releases, then clears this list.

- [Buffy] Chat controls: every finished reply has **Do it** and **Type it**, and the composer’s keyboard button types your draft exactly into the app behind Izuki without sending it. “Do it” now bypasses the study coach and reaches the screen agent, while the graded-writing and never-submit rules still apply. (Production build, 47 conversation checks and `cargo check --lib` passed; native click/type still needs a packaged-app smoke test.)
- [Freebuff] Nova Notch + Shape Studio: a glass notch that attaches to the top of the screen with two curved side scoops, a compact live status card, and a real step ring, expanding into the existing Island when you need it. Classic stays the default. Shape Studio adds live opacity, blur, accent, shadow and calm-motion controls, an optional brighter Light glass finish, and an opt-in “Expand while a task runs”. The open panel now caps its height on short screens.
- [Freebuff] Chat: “answer it”, “give ans”, “answer question 5” and “answer all of them” now answer the question on screen instead of replying with a Study-log refusal. The answer replaces the waiting assistant bubble instead of adding a duplicate.
- [Claude] New PowerPoint decks: asking Izuki to write a new .pptx now makes a real deck (one slide per "# " title, "- " bullets under it; long slides shrink their text to fit) instead of "I can't make a slide deck yet". The storage report names a folder you can clear the way you'd find it ("Downloads › project › target") instead of a full C:\Users path. Two small review fixes from 1.0.215: a replaced task's direct-step results stay with their own task, and `npm run trim:cache` also trims builds made for an explicit target. Plus docs/IPHONE-ROADMAP.md: what an iPhone companion can and can't do, in order. (The decks were checked by Izuki's own validator and opened with python-pptx; opening one in PowerPoint itself is still to be tried on a PC that has it.)
- [Claude, with Freebuff's follow-ups on the same branch — PR #108] Android companion: "Hey Nova" reads the microphone quietly and only asks Android's speech service when someone speaks (no restart loop, no repeated connect sound; backs off, stops with a reason, steps aside while Izuki is open). Phone readiness shows Android's real answer per item: Android control connected vs only switched on, restricted settings detected where Android says so (with Samsung's "Allow restricted settings" steps), the orb as an accessibility overlay (no "Appear on top"), microphone, notifications (asked with Android's own prompt), background use. The orb rests instead of claiming "Listening…", can be dragged and long-pressed away, and can't stay on "Working…" if the page stops. Taps by label reach labels inside rows; gestures report Android's own completed/cancelled; typing is read back; Autopilot waits for the app to really open, never presses Voice search, and only counts Enter when the screen changed. Requests have a deadline, a live stage with seconds, and Send becomes Stop. Diagnostics (Settings → Phone) with a copyable report. Top buttons all take the Call button's oval. Not verified on a physical phone yet.
- [Claude] Browser extension 1.6.4: an AI tab lock records what it's busy with; a busy tab offers "Stop that one and ask this" and "Show Gemini"; a frozen tab or an endless answer ends with a reason (per-call limit and a hard deadline); a stale lock clears itself; a panel reopened while a question still runs shows it with Stop.
- [Claude] Windows: hardware requests start from facts (maker, model, maker apps from the registry). A battery charge limit goes to the maker's app (Lenovo Commercial Vantage on the owner's ThinkPad T14) instead of Windows Settings. After a failed step the next attempt isn't announced as progress, and the stop message says what happened in plain words instead of "A step keeps failing after fresh looks".
- [Claude] Android voice match (optional, off until taught): "Teach my voice" takes three short recordings; "Hey Nova" then only starts things when the wake phrase sounds like the taught voice (the same WavLM model and two-of-three rule as the PC). The wake service hands the phrase audio to the page on Android 13+; otherwise the first spoken command is checked. Only a voice template is kept on the phone. The voice model (about 95 MB) downloads the first time. Not verified on a physical phone.

- Stop also cancels app requests waiting for a new Composio session. Spoken wakes preserve their audio through overlapping polls and require a voice check before using inline commands; transcription timeouts explain the delay and resume an active call without resuming after Stop.
