# Waiting for the next release

Codex and Freebuff: add one line per change you pushed. Write it in plain
words for the owner, and put your name in brackets. Claude moves these lines
into the next `docs/RELEASE-x.y.z.md` when it releases, then clears this list.

- [Claude] Android: replies are spoken with Android's own voice (the app's web view had no working voice, so many replies were silent). Calls in the app listen with Android's own speech recognition (quiet speech was being dropped).
- [Claude] Android: a real phone agent. "What's taking space on my phone", "turn on dark mode", "open YouTube and play lofi" are done on the phone, step by checked step; it never presses call, send, pay, buy, delete or reset buttons, and never acts while locked. Phone jobs no longer get "connect your PC".
- [Claude] Android: likely root cause of "Hey Nova does nothing": the app never declared Android's speech service, which Android 11+ hides without it. Fixed; not yet tried on the owner's Samsung.
- [Claude] Android: the orb only shows while Izuki listens, works or talks (keeping a resting orb is a setting); tap it to talk, tap again to stop, tap its words to open Izuki. iPhone-only settings no longer show on Android. One-tap controls: labels fit their tiles, plus flashlight, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, volume, split screen, battery, storage, camera and Do not disturb.

- [Claude] Faster answers: Gemini 3 is told not to think first the way it accepts, Google Search is only attached when the question needs it, a stale connection is retried after 12 s, and the connection is warmed when Izuki comes back or you start typing.

The Screen notch now has the laptop-notch shape (top corners curving out into the screen edge) and only comes down from the top middle.
