“Hey Siri, Izuki” (iPhone — optional)

✅ Easiest way (no setup): just open the Izuki app from your home screen and tap Call — talk hands-free. You don't need anything below. The steps below are ONLY if you also want to start it by saying “Hey Siri”.

Optional: this makes “Hey Siri, Izuki, what's the weather” open it with your words. Do it slowly, once, and skip it if it's confusing:

  1. Open Apple's Shortcuts app (it's built into every iPhone).
  2. Tap the + at the top right → then Add Action.
  3. In the search box type Dictate Text → tap it. (This makes Siri listen to what you say.)
  4. Tap + Add Action again → search Text → tap Text. In that text box, paste this exactly:
  5. Now, right after the link you pasted (no space), tap the little “Dictated Text” chip that appears above the keyboard — so the line reads …?q= then Dictated Text.
  6. Tap + Add Action again → search Open URLs → tap it. Where it says “URL”, tap it and pick the Text from step 4–5.
  7. Tap the shortcut's name at the top → rename it to just Izuki → Done.

Now say “Hey Siri, Izuki”, then what you want. (Optional: Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap to run it by tapping the back of your phone.)

Link your PC (optional)

Have Izuki on a Windows PC? In the PC app turn on Call Izuki from your phone and paste its link here. Then “play lofi on my PC”, your email and calendar, and anything else on the PC work from here too.

Your accounts (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Slack, GitHub, Blackboard…) run through that PC link, because it holds the apps Izuki is allowed into. Ask here and it goes straight there — if an app isn’t linked yet you’ll get a Connect button to sign in. Free: your own free key on the PC, nothing hosted.
