Alarms (iPhone — one-time setup)

Say "wake me up at 7" and Izuki gives you a button that puts it straight into your Clock. On iPhone that needs one small shortcut, made once:

  1. Open Shortcuts, tap +, name it Izuki Alarm.
  2. Add Split Text → split Shortcut Input by Custom |.
  3. Add Get Item from List → First Item.
  4. Add Create Alarm (Clock) → time: Item from List. Tap Done.

Android needs nothing — the button opens your Clock with the time filled in. Either way there's also an "Add to calendar" button that always works.

Drive mode

Tap Drive at the top (or say “drive mode”): Izuki becomes your co-pilot, hands-free. “Take me to…” starts Google Maps' turn-by-turn; “find gas near me”, “play music”, “text that I'm on my way” are done at once, and “how long is left” gets the real distance and drive time for the place you named. It speaks up after two hours at the wheel and when your battery runs low. 🎉 Fun mode makes it a cheeky road-trip buddy.

Your speed and the trip line come from your phone's location, while Drive mode is on only. To say what's left, Izuki looks the place you named up on OpenStreetMap and asks its free router for the remaining distance and drive time — the road's usual time, not live traffic — sending only that place name and where you are; nothing is stored.

  2. Tap the + at the top right → then Add Action.
  3. In the search box type Dictate Text → tap it. (This makes Siri listen to what you say.)
  4. Add URL Encode for the dictated text (so spaces and “&” are preserved), then add a Text action. In that text box, paste this prefix:
  5. Right after the prefix (no space), insert the URL Encoded Text variable — so the line reads …?q= then the encoded text.
