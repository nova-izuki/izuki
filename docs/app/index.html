Izuki settings

1. Your free AI key

Izuki runs on Google's free Gemini. Get a key (sign in → Create API key), copy it, and paste it here. It stays on this phone only.

1 · Get a free Gemini key ↗

2. Put Izuki on your home screen

iPhone: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen. It opens like an app.

3. “Hey Siri, Izuki” (iPhone, optional)

In the Shortcuts app: tap +, name it Izuki, then add two actions:

  1. Dictate Text
  2. Open URLs with and tap the end to insert Dictated Text

Say “Hey Siri, Izuki”, then what you want — Izuki opens and answers. You can also put it on the Action Button or Back Tap (Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap).

4. Link your PC (optional)

Have Izuki on a Windows PC? In the PC app turn on Call Izuki from your phone and paste its link here. Then “play lofi on my PC”, your email and calendar, and anything else on the PC work from here too.

What Izuki remembers

Free and private: your key, chats, memories and reminders are kept on this phone. Messages go straight to Google's Gemini (and to your own PC if linked) — nowhere else.
