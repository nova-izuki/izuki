# Izuki 1.0.87: any camera, 3D mouths that open, and proof PC fast is working

## Any camera, picked for you
The Island's camera now works with **any** webcam: built-in, USB (Logitech, Razer, Elgato, Anker and more), capture cards and virtual cameras like OBS.
- **It picks the right one by itself:** the one you chose last time if it's still plugged in, otherwise an external webcam over the built-in one.
- **If a camera won't start,** it moves on to the next one.
- **If you unplug the one showing,** it switches to another by itself.
- With more than one camera, a picker appears in the corner of the camera view.

## 3D faces really talk
The built-in 3D faces (the holograms, the Woman and the Man) now open their mouths when they talk: a dark opening with the top teeth showing, not just lips moving. Their blinks from 1.0.85 stay.

## See that "Keep my PC fast 24/7" is working
With 24/7 on, PC Care now shows it live: "● Running — checked 3s ago · last clean 2 h ago: cleared 858 MB of old temp files". It was working before (it cleared 858 MB on its own last night); now you can see it.
