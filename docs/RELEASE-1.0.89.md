# Izuki 1.0.89: a faster PC, the always-on desktop character is off

## The desktop character is off
The character that stood on your desktop all the time (from 1.0.86) is switched off for everyone. It redrew itself and checked the PC many times a second, which slowed some PCs down, and it wasn't what characters were meant to be.

You can still turn it back on in Settings → Desktop mate.

## Coming next
Characters as a look for Izuki, just like the orbs: they show up only when you ask something, can be closed, zoomed and minimized, and move like a real person (gestures, mouth and body while explaining, walking to what they're working on). The pet becomes optional, and it moves too.
