# Izuki 1.0.24

Izuki moves in at the top of your screen.

## The Island

- **A little pill at the top of your screen**, like a phone's live
  activities. It shows what Izuki is doing right now, what's playing on your
  PC (with the cover), your next reminder, and a "Finished" tick when a job
  is done.
- **Push your mouse to the top of the screen** (or rest it on the pill) and
  it opens: a big **Stop** button while Izuki works, ⏮ ⏯ ⏭ for your music,
  and big **Talk**, **Draw** and **Open Izuki** buttons.
- **It has a face.** Izuki's liquid orb with eyes that follow your mouse,
  blink, dance while music plays — and squish if you poke it (keep poking
  and it gets dizzy).
- **Never in the way.** It steps aside for full-screen films and games, and
  quick clicks pass straight through it, so it never steals a click meant
  for a browser tab underneath.
- Don't want it? Switch it off in **Talk → Island at the top of the screen**.

## Music behaves

- **One song at a time.** Asking for a song pauses whatever else is playing
  first (Spotify, another tab…).
- **"Pause" never starts music, and "play" never stops it.** They used to
  press the play/pause key, which flips whatever state you're in. Now they
  set it — tested on a real player: pause → paused, pause again → still
  paused, play → playing.
