# Izuki 1.0.59

## Screenshots and screen recording
- "Take a screenshot" saves it to Pictures › Screenshots and copies it, ready to paste.
- "Record my screen" starts a recording; "stop recording" saves it to Videos › Izuki Recordings and shows it in File Explorer. Recording uses FFmpeg, a free tool; the first time, Izuki offers to install it. Recordings stop by themselves after an hour.

## YouTube, straight away
- "Open Chrome and play how to make money, skip the ads", "watch lofi in the browser" and "search YouTube for cat videos" now go straight to YouTube, with no thinking time. It plays the best match and skips the ads.
- Opening Chrome, Edge or Brave goes straight into the account you used last, never the "Who's using Chrome?" screen that Izuki used to get stuck on.

## Dre and Nia
- Two new characters under Accents: **Dre** and **Nia**. They talk with real Black American slang: smooth, funny and real, never a caricature.
- With "Match voice and character" on, characters bring up the face that fits them.
- The Man face can now wear every haircut: locs, box braids, quiff, curly-top fade and more.

## Easier to get around
- The jump bar (with **Top**) is on the Draw and Apps pages too, not just Settings.
- If you drag the Izuki window partly off the screen, so the chat box is hidden under the taskbar, it slides back into view when you let go.
