# Izuki 1.0.143: the pen cleans up after itself, and reviews go into your notes

## 🖊️ The pen no longer stays on the wrong answer
While Izuki explains, it circles and underlines things on your screen. Those marks were only cleared when you started a new task or pressed Esc. So on a quiz review, after Izuki pressed Next, the last question's marks sat on top of the new question's answers, out of line.
- Marks are wiped **the moment Izuki clicks, scrolls, types or presses a key**, because the page under them is about to change.
- After an answer, they go **a few seconds after Izuki finishes speaking**.
- As a safety net, no mark stays up longer than **two minutes**.

## 📝 "Review all the questions and save them to my notes" really saves them
Izuki explained each question but never saved anything. Now, when you ask it to review or go through something **and save it to your notes**:
- every explanation it gives along the way is added to one note, **"Review — <the page>"**, in Izuki Notes, as it goes;
- at the end it tells you how many it saved, so you can study them (flashcards and "quiz me" work on that note too);
- reviews and explanations are never held back to space out progress lines. Each one is said.

## 🎓 Notes to study, not answers to replay
Izuki's long-term memory no longer keeps quiz or test answers to give back later. Your Izuki Notes are where the reviews go, so you can learn them yourself before a test.

## 🏝️ The Island: move it any time, clear its history
- **The move handle stays at the top** of the open Island, even while you scroll down a long answer. Before, it scrolled away with the page, so once you'd asked something there was nothing to grab.
- **Clear history:** the saved-chats list (the 🕘 button) has a **Clear history** button. Tap it twice to clear every chat you haven't pinned. Pinned ones stay.

## 🧹 Housekeeping
- **"Do not write it in my notes"** and other ways of saying no are always respected.
- Quiz answers that were remembered before this version are never used either.
