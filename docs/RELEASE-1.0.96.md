# Izuki 1.0.96: glowing screen edges

## A line of light around your screen
While Izuki is with you, a thin, bright line runs around the edges of your screen, with a soft glow reaching in. It shows what Izuki is doing:
- **Listening:** calm cyan, drifting slowly.
- **Thinking:** every colour (cyan, violet, pink, amber, teal) flowing around the screen faster.
- **Talking:** the glow swells with each word.
- **Quiet:** it fades away, and nothing keeps running.

Izuki's own colours, not Apple's. It's light on the PC: the colours only slide (no blur effects), and it isn't there at all while Izuki is quiet.

## Your choice
In Settings → Look:
- **Glowing screen edges:** on or off.
- **Glow only:** hides the orb or character, so the glowing edges alone show Izuki is listening and talking. Captions still show.

## On the phone too
The phone app gets the same glow while Izuki listens, thinks and talks, with its own on/off switch under Your orb.
