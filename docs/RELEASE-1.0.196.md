# Izuki 1.0.196: real Office files, safer saves, and a browser link only Izuki can use

Izuki makes real Word and Excel files and edits copies instead of your originals, saves can never leave half a file, practice quizzes can be done together, and the browser extension and Izuki now prove themselves to each other.

## 📄 Word, Excel and PowerPoint

- Ask for a report as a **Word file** or a table as an **Excel file**, and Izuki makes a real .docx or .xlsx. Before, text saved under those names made a file Office couldn't open.
- Ask it to change a line in your document, add a paragraph, or set a cell in a spreadsheet: the change goes into a **copy** named "(Izuki edit)". Your original is never touched, and you press Allow first.
- Izuki reads Excel by cell (B4, C12), PDFs, and OpenDocument files (.odt, .ods, .odp).

## 💾 Safer saves

- Every save is all or nothing: a crash or a full disk never leaves half a file, and your old version stays whole until the new one is complete.
- Each save keeps its own backup ("(before Izuki)", "(before Izuki 2)"…), so a second save never overwrites the first backup.
- Izuki won't write plain text over a photo, a song, an archive or a program.

## 📝 Practice quizzes, done together

- On a page that says it's practice (practice quiz, self-check, ungraded), Izuki can pick answers with you and go to the next question. You press Submit.
- Graded quizzes and tests stay hands-off: Izuki explains each question and suggests an answer with the reason, and you choose. When it can't tell, it treats the page as graded.
- Instead of just saying no, it says what it *can* do on that page.

## 🧩 Browser extension 1.6.0

- **Quick, Think deeper and Research** in the side panel. Research searches the web (and the page, if you share it) and writes an answer with its sources, showing each step.
- **Izuki and the extension now prove themselves to each other** with a private key made just for you. No other program on your PC, even one under another Windows account, can pose as either to read your pages, click in your browser or start a task on your PC.
- **If you added the extension by hand** (from the website's zip), set it up once from Izuki: **Settings → Browser extension → Set up in this browser**. Added by hand, it still works with AI websites, but not with Izuki on your PC.

## ⬆️ Updates

- **Go back to the previous version** in Settings → Updates. It's downloaded and signature-checked like any update.
- Updates check that the version written in the signature matches the one announced.
- The installer waits for Izuki to close before it copies anything.

## Behind the scenes

- Every change in this release was reviewed by CodeRabbit (ten rounds) and checked by tests, including the browser extension in a real Edge.
- The Android companion is ready to move to a private signing key. Nothing changes on your phone yet.
