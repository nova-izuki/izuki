# Izuki 1.0.118: 🌱 Touch Grass, and Air control that leaves resting hands alone

## 🌱 Touch Grass: your PC's work, from your phone
A new **Grass** button in the phone app, next to Remote.
- **Your missions, with their real results:** Verified, Partly done, Blocked, Failed or Needs you, with what Izuki actually found.
- **Add a mission from your phone** and **start them**. Izuki does them one by one, and your PC stays awake while it works.
- **⏹ Stop everything**: missions, whatever Izuki is doing on the PC, and its work in your apps.
- **Waiting for your yes:** when Izuki has an email, message or post ready, the exact draft shows here, with who it's to, the subject and the words.
  - **Approve** sends exactly that draft, once, from the same account. It only says "Sent" when the app confirms it.
  - **Reject** drops it, and nothing is sent. One tap decides: both buttons lock so it can't be both.
  - **Nothing waits forever.** After 30 minutes a draft expires unsent, because silence is never a yes.
  - **The phone buzzes** when a new one arrives while the page is open.
- **It tells you when your PC is locked:** anything that needs the screen waits, but email, apps and files still work.
- **🖥️ Watch** jumps to the live view of your PC (Remote).
- **Only a phone you paired with this PC** can open it (the same pass as Remote). It never moves your PC's mouse. Every approval, mission and stop is written to the remote audit log on the PC.

## ✋ Air control: resting hands are left alone
Leaning your cheek on your hand, or resting it on the desk, used to get read as gestures, even after pausing. Here's what changed:
- **Pause and un-pause are a deliberate move:** show an **open hand, then close it into a fist** and hold it for a second. A hand that's just resting curled, which is what kept un-pausing Air control, never counts.
- **Only a hand facing the camera drives the pointer.** A hand on its side (on your chin or cheek, or resting on its edge) is ignored.
- **A hand held still for 4 seconds stops clicking** until you move it again ("Resting — move to carry on"), so leaning on your hand doesn't click anything.
- **Checked with the recorded test videos:** a raised hand still drives the pointer, typing hands are still ignored, and there were no accidental clicks.
