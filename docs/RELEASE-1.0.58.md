# Izuki 1.0.58

## Smarter about what's on your screen
- **Reads behind the links.** Ask a question about a page and Izuki also reads the most related pages it links to, such as the syllabus or the assignment details. It reads them quietly through your browser with your own sign-ins, so school and work sites work. It never opens links like Log out, Delete or Buy. You need the Izuki browser extension.
- **Knows how each app works.** Built-in playbooks give Izuki each app's own shortcuts and how an expert works it: FL Studio, Ableton, Premiere, CapCut, Photoshop, Blender, Figma, VS Code, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, browsers, Spotify, Discord and school sites. In apps that draw their own screen, like FL Studio, it uses keys and menus instead of dragging by pixels. Ask "what are the shortcuts here?" for a quick list.
- **Knows where things sit.** Every button now comes with the part of the app it's in, so a sidebar link isn't mistaken for the question.
- **Faster clicks.** "Click settings" finds "Preferences": a quick text-only pick from the app's own controls, with no screenshot. Final buttons (Delete, Send, Buy) are never pressed on a guess.

## Beat starter
- "Make me an Afrobeats drum pattern at 108 BPM" writes a real MIDI drum groove.
- 17 genres: Afrobeats, Amapiano, Afro house, Highlife, Trap, Drill, Boom bap, Lo-fi, R&B, Gospel, House, Reggaeton, Dancehall, Drum & bass, Jersey club, Pop and Rock.
- Each groove is swung and slightly off the grid like a real player, and ends with a fill.
- It's saved in Music › Izuki Beats, ready to drag into FL Studio or any music app. Works from your phone too.

## Browser side panel
- **Read linked pages too** is now part of the side panel: answers can come from pages the current page links to.

## Updates
- When a new version is ready, the Island shows **Update now** and your phone gets a message.
- Izuki checks every 2 hours.
- New versions now publish by themselves, so the website's Download button always has the newest one.

## For developers
- **@izuki/app-playbooks**: how desktop apps really work, as plain JSON (MIT), for any AI agent. Add your own playbooks to Izuki's data folder under `playbooks`.
