# Izuki 1.0.98: Lucía and Captain Salty, painted and animated

Lucía and Captain Salty were the last two characters still drawn the old way. They now match the other 35:
- **Same painted style:** the same look as everyone else.
- **Frame-by-frame animation:** they walk (Salty's long coat sways as he goes), talk with their hands, listen, think with a hand on their chin, type on a little laptop, wave, give a thumbs up, cheer, point and shrug.
- **Same size as the rest:** whole heads in every pose, standing straight.
- **Bending puppet too:** each has the bending puppet the others use as a fallback.

That makes 37 animated characters. Pick one in Settings → Look.
