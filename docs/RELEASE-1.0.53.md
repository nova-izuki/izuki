# Izuki 1.0.53

Phone calls with Izuki now flow like a real conversation.

## Hands-free, every turn
- After each reply Izuki goes straight back to listening and notices when you've finished talking. You no longer need to tap the orb.
- The meter that hears you speak has its own audio engine. If the phone puts it to sleep (often after a reply plays), it wakes it up again. If it ever hears perfect silence, it rebuilds itself.

## Faster replies
- Izuki starts talking as soon as the first sentence of its answer is written. It doesn't wait for the whole answer or the whole recording.
- The rest of the answer is prepared while the first sentence plays, so the voice runs on without a gap. That's still at most two voice requests a reply, so free keys last.
- On a call, answers are kept to a few spoken sentences.

## Captions that follow the voice
- On the call screen, the words light up as Izuki says them and long answers scroll along. This works with both the Gemini voice and the phone's own voice.
