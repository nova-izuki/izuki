What it can do

Talk to it. It does the rest.

Everything a smart friend at your shoulder would do — for free, on your own PC.

🎙️

Hands-free, like a real conversation

Say your wake word and talk like you would to a person. Cut in any time. It turns your music down while you talk, hears names right, and keeps listening for what's next.

🖱️

Does the work

Opens apps, searches, clicks, types, scrolls straight to the right link, skips ads — looking again until it's really done.

✏️

Teaches with a pen

“Explain this maths problem.” It pauses the video, then circles, underlines and writes notes on your screen as it explains.

👀

Sees what you see

“What does this error mean?” It reads your screen — and the app's hidden buttons — to answer precisely.

✍️

Draw to show it

Hold Ctrl+D, circle anything, and ask. One tap to click, copy or explain it.

💬

Chat, reminders & memory

A chat that never touches your screen, reminders that ring on your PC and phone, and a memory of you that you can see and wipe any time.

🔌

In your apps

Email, calendar, Drive, Slack, Notion and hundreds more with your own free key. It always shows the draft and asks before sending.

Get started

Up and running in two minutes.

No complicated setup. If you can install an app, you can use Izuki.

1

Download & install

Grab the installer and run it. (Windows may say it's a new app — click More info → Run anyway.)

2

Paste a free key

The welcome tour links you to a free Google Gemini key. Paste it in — that's the only setup.

3

Start talking

Hold the talk hotkey and speak, or type. Add a free wake word like “Hey Nova” to go fully hands-free.

Free, for real

No subscription. No catch.

Izuki runs on your own PC with your own free AI key, so there's nothing to charge you for. Your screen and voice go only to the AI you choose — nowhere else.
