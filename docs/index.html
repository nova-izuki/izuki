Pause a task, then pick it up

Every task card has Pause: the step that's running finishes, then Izuki waits for you. Use your PC, press Resume, and it takes a fresh look before the next step, so it never clicks where things used to be. After a scroll, a shortcut or opening an app it checks that something really happened.

A storage checkup you can actually read

Tap PC checkup in the Chat tab and get a card, not a wall of text: each drive's bar, the biggest apps, folders and files, and what's safe to clear. It's measured on your PC, and nothing is deleted unless you say so.

Hand or Eye control, right from the chat

Pick Hand or Eye control, see every hand move, and stop Air at once from the chat or the orb. Switching from Eye to Hand lets go of anything it was holding. Eye control stays off unless you turn it on.

Arrange your windows with one sentence

Say “put my windows side by side”, “tile my windows” or “focus layout”, or tap it in Style → Layouts. Up to two windows go side by side and up to four in a grid or columns; the one you used last gets the biggest spot, and the rest stay where they are. “Undo the layout” puts them all back. Save a setup with “save my windows as Work”, and “set up my Work layout” brings back every window on every screen, opening the apps that aren't running. (Windows won't let Izuki move an app run as administrator; it says so.)

Describe a vibe, get a desktop

Type or say “make my PC look cozy”, “cyberpunk” or “black and gold” and Izuki picks the closest of its 19 looks, with matching tweaks if you want them. For deeper changes, Style → Tweaks helps you add Windhawk and points to the mods worth starting with. Changed your mind? “Undo the tweaks” puts back exactly what a tweak pack changed.

Your data, in your hands

Settings → Privacy shows everything Izuki can reach, from the mic to your linked accounts, each with an off switch. “What Izuki did” lists every click, app and change it made on your PC (or ask “what did you do today?”). Take a copy of all your data in one file, keys left out, or erase everything in one go.

A free NVIDIA brain, set up for you

In Settings, Izuki walks you to NVIDIA's free key page, says plainly what the free tier means, then tries NVIDIA's free models on your own key and picks the ones that fit: one that can see your screen to do things, and a fast one for chat.

Chat history, pins, and tasks that wait for you

Your chats are saved on your PC: search them, pick one up again, and pin the ones that matter. A task cut short, even by a restart, resumes when you say “keep going”, and a step that didn't really work is fixed instead of reported as done. Eye Control gets a native emergency stop (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+X) and stays off unless you turn it on.

One assistant for the whole desktop.

Voice, screen awareness, automation, teaching and PC care — built to act only when you ask.

Hands-free, like a real conversation

Say your wake word and talk like you would to a person. Cut in any time. It turns your music down while you talk, hears names right, and keeps listening for what's next.

Does the work

Opens apps, searches, clicks, types, scrolls straight to the right link, skips ads — looking again until it's really done.

A classroom on your screen

Pause a lesson, keep it playing or slow to 0.75×. Izuki explains visible details with pen marks. Follow a lesson for up to five explanations in the Izuki browser.

Sees what you see

“What does this error mean?” It reads your screen — and the app's hidden buttons — to answer precisely.

Draw to show it

Hold Ctrl+D, circle anything, and ask. One tap to click, copy or explain it.

Chat, reminders & memory

A chat that never touches your screen, reminders that ring on your PC and phone, and a memory of you that you can see and wipe any time.

In your apps

Search and connect Gmail, Microsoft, work, code, files and supported socials from one Apps tab. Every account uses its own approval, and Izuki still asks before sending.

Heads-ups

A new important email, a meeting in 15 minutes, homework due tomorrow — it taps you on the shoulder, on your PC and your phone. Plus a morning brief.

Searches the web for you

“What's the latest on…?” It searches and reads the pages itself, quietly — even school sites you're signed in to, in its own little browser.

Your files & a real terminal

In the chat it finds and reads your files (Word, PDF, PowerPoint too), writes files, and runs real commands — zip, install, run a script, even Python — like a coding assistant. A Ask first or Auto‑run switch, like Claude Code, and it always shows you the exact command. Your keys stay off‑limits.

Companion on your phone

Use Izuki on any phone from the web app today. The native Android companion adds opt-in Home, Back, Recents, Notifications and Quick Settings; the iPhone companion brings conversations, reminders and Siri handoff within iOS rules. Free core companion.

30+ voices & characters

Warm Nova, Atlanta’s Dre, Houston’s Nia, deep calm Leo, British Sophie, Spanish Lucía, a hype coach, a butler, a pirate — or Rex, unfiltered and savage, if you dare. US English sits beside British, Nigerian, Spanish, French and more. Rename it, change the voice, speed and pitch, and give it your own personality.

Sounds like a person

Natural voices in US English and more, breathing at commas and pausing at periods — free, no key. Or Google's Gemini voice (your Gemini key), Groq, Azure and ElevenLabs for the most human sound. Offline voice on your PC too, in English, Spanish, French, Swahili, Hindi and more.

Two ways to take control

Mouse mode moves a visible hand like a person would. Jarvis mode presses buttons and types into boxes straight through Windows — no pointer flying around, faster, and your own mouse stays free. It checks the real control before acting and never guesses.

Your companion, your style

Liquid glass, Clear water, Pure water, Ferrofluid, Stardust, a Hologram face, Tidal pearl and Star crystal — real 3D light on your graphics card, on PC and phone. Try them above ↑

Keep your flows tidy

Clear matching flows or start fresh, then Undo if you change your mind. Exact repeat recordings reuse the saved flow, and video explanations stay out of your library.

Instant commands

Say “scroll down”, “louder”, “pause”, “next song”, “go back”, “new tab” or “minimize this” and it just happens — no AI, no waiting, like Siri. It runs the whole PC too: “set the volume to 30”, “snap left”, “lock my pc”, “take a screenshot”, “open task manager”, and “what time is it” answered straight from the clock — free and instant, with nothing for a model to get wrong.

Works with any AI — even small free ones

Big AIs get the full playbook. Small free ones get Easy Mode: a simple menu, and Izuki finds the real button by its name — so they do the job instead of explaining it. If an AI starts rambling, Izuki ignores it and waits for one that actually acts.

Useful diagnostics, clear reporting

Desktop errors are logged locally. Something wrong? shows whether an automatic reporting service is configured; you can also review and submit an issue on GitHub. Central reporting is not enabled in this release.

Set up in a minute

Copy a free key on the website that opens, come back — Izuki fills it in by itself. Tap Gmail and it walks you through the rest. No settings to hunt for.

What it will never do on its own

Izuki drives your real mouse and keyboard, so these are built into the code, not left to the AI's judgement. It won't type into a password box — it asks the box what it is and refuses, and it never reads one either. It won't act on your lock screen. It won't press anything you can't take back — Submit, Send, Pay, Buy, Delete, Erase, Format, Share, Invite, Close account — unless you asked for that in so many words. It won't pick or submit answers on a quiz; it explains and points, and you choose. It won't type a command into your terminal unless you spelled it out.

One key stops everything

Press Esc and Izuki lets go instantly — the task, the voice, the lot. Ctrl+Shift+Q works any time. Go on, try it on this page.

Is it safe?

Built to be trusted with your PC.

An assistant that clicks and types for you has to earn that. Here's exactly how Izuki treats your screen, your keys and your things — and where you can check every word of it yourself.

Open source, every line

Izuki is MIT-licensed and all of its code is public. What it does with your screen, your voice and your keys is there for anyone to read and check.

Read the code ↗

Your AI, your key

There's no Izuki account. Your screen and your words go from your PC to the AI service you picked, using your own free key. Hearing you and reading text off the screen happen on your PC (unless you switch on Sharper hearing).

What leaves your PC

It asks before anything final

Send, Pay, Buy, Delete, Submit: never pressed unless you asked for exactly that. It won't type into a password box or act on your lock screen, and it won't do graded quizzes or tests for you.

What it never does

You're always in charge

Esc stops everything at once and Ctrl+Shift+Q works any time. Pause a task between steps, take over, and carry on. Settings → Privacy lists what Izuki can reach and everything it did, and copies or erases all your data. Eye control is off unless you turn it on, with its own emergency stop.

Your controls

No ads, no trackers

No sign-up and no ads in the app. This website has no analytics or tracking cookies. Error reports stay on your PC; nothing is sent to us automatically.

Questions

The short answers.

What to know before you install.

Is Izuki really free?

Yes. Izuki is free and MIT-licensed. It runs with your own free AI key from Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter or Mistral, or a model on your own PC. AI providers may apply their own usage limits.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC and an internet connection for the AI service. You will need a free Google Gemini key, or another supported provider key. There is no Izuki account.

Does it work on a Mac or phone?

The desktop app is for Windows. The phone companion is a free web app for iPhone and Android, with native Android controls in preview. Mac and Linux are not supported yet; the browser extension works in Chrome and Edge.

Which AI does it use?

Desktop: Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, Meta Llama, or a local model. In the browser extension, you can also route questions to the AI tab you already use, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Perplexity.

Is it safe to let it control my PC?

Your keys stay on your devices. It will not type into password fields, act on a locked screen, or press Send, Pay, Buy or Delete unless you specifically asked. Esc stops everything, and the source is open to inspection.

Does it record my screen all the time?

No. Izuki captures the screen only when a task needs it and you have asked for help. Hearing and screen-text recognition happen on your PC. Nothing is continuously uploaded to us, and the site has no analytics or tracking cookies.
