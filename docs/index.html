The AI that lives on your screen.

Say “Hey Nova” and just talk. Izuki sees your screen, answers you out loud, and uses your mouse and keyboard to get things done — like having a friend who's great with computers, right inside your PC.

Play something chill on YouTube

Talk to it. It does the rest.

Not a chatbot in a box — a companion that can actually use your computer.

🎙️

Hands-free voice

Say your wake word, then talk like you would to a person. Interrupt it any time. It keeps listening for your next question.

👀

Sees your screen

“What does this error mean?” “Summarise this page.” It looks at what you're looking at and explains it.

🖱️

Does the work

Opens apps, searches, clicks, types, scrolls, skips ads — look, act, look again, until it's actually done.

👉

Points things out

“Where's the export button?” It circles it right on your screen while it explains — great for learning any app.

✍️

Draw to show it

Hold Ctrl+D, circle anything, and ask about it. Or let it ask you to circle the right one when it's unsure.

🧠

Remembers you

Your name, your preferences, the habits it notices. You can see and delete every memory, any time.

Just say it.

“Open Spotify and play something chill.” “What's on my screen?” “Reply to this email saying I'll be late.” “Explain this chart to me.” “Point at the save button.” “Find me a cheap flight to Lagos.” “Remember I use my work Chrome profile.” “Keep going.”

Up and running in two minutes.

No complicated setup. If you can install an app, you can use Izuki.

Download & install

Grab the installer and run it. (Windows may say it's a new app — click More info → Run anyway.)

Paste a free key

The welcome tour links you to a free Google Gemini key. Paste it in — that's the only setup.

Start talking

Hold the talk hotkey and speak, or type in the chat. Add a free wake word like “Hey Nova” to go fully hands-free.
