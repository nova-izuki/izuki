# Izuki 1.0.195: it knows what you've handed in

Izuki already read your due dates from Blackboard. Now it also reads whether each one is handed in, from the same grade records "what are my grades?" uses: graded, submitted and waiting to be graded, or excused all count as done.

## ✓ Done and not done

- **"What haven't I done?"**, "which assignments are done and not done?", "did I submit the lab?": what's past due and not handed in, what's still to do, and how many are already in.
- **"What's due this week?"** marks each item **✓ handed in** or **(not handed in yet)**.
- **"What's overdue?"** lists only what isn't handed in. It used to say the calendar can't tell whether you submitted, and list everything.
- **"What should I do first?"** skips what's already in.
- **Heads-ups and the morning brief** no longer remind you about work you've already handed in. (One of the reasons eight "due today" alerts arrived at once.)

When Blackboard doesn't show an item either way, Izuki says it can't tell instead of guessing. That happens with work done on another site, like a uCertify or publisher lab, until that site reports the result back to Blackboard. A school calendar link (.ics) alone can't show submissions, so with only a calendar link everything is listed as before.

You can ask in the Chat tab, out loud, or from your phone, and the chat's AI can look it up too.
