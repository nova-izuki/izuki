# Izuki 1.0.137: it follows the conversation

## 🧵 "Find that one" now means the one you were just talking about
Izuki has two parts: one that chats and one that works your screen. The chat part remembered the whole conversation, but the screen part only got your latest sentence.

So if you asked "which webcam fits my ThinkPad?" and then said "open Amazon and find that one", the screen part had no idea which one you meant. Answers felt like they didn't follow what you'd just said.

Now, when you ask Izuki to do something that leans on what came before ("it", "that one", "the same", "again", or any short follow-up), the screen part gets the last few lines of your conversation too. That includes what Izuki itself told you. It works from the phone as well.

A long, brand-new request still stands on its own, so old topics don't creep in.

Together with 1.0.135 (lines take turns instead of cutting each other off, and no more "I already broke that down"), conversations should now flow.
