# Izuki 1.0.224: Izuki's own window, whole moves, and a grid for games and drawing

## ⚡ On your PC

- **Izuki works in its own window.** When a task needs a web page, Izuki opens it in a separate browser window that doesn't take your focus, and works there through the page itself: never your mouse, never your keyboard. Keep using your PC while it works. A live picture of that window shows in the task panel, and when the task is done the window comes to the front with the result. (Needs the Izuki browser extension 1.6.5: Izuki offers the update.)
- **Whole moves, not one click per look.** Izuki now does everything it can already see in one go (types and presses Enter, fills every field of a form it can see) before looking again, so tasks need fewer AI rounds and finish sooner. Typing a search always runs it straight away.
- **A grid for games and drawing apps.** In apps with no buttons Izuki can read (a game, a drawing app, a map), it draws thin grid lines with numbered rulers on what it sees, so it clicks exactly where it means to instead of guessing.

## 📱 On the phone

- The phone can answer at the length you want: **Quick**, **Normal** or **Deep**, one tap above the box you type in, and the setting sticks. For a question on another app's screen, "Answer for me" (off by default) reads it and gives you the answer here in the chat. It never types or taps an answer into the app. [Freebuff, Claude]
- Every AI in Settings has a working link to its own key page (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, Meta), opening in the phone's browser. [Freebuff]
- The command centre scrolls on a short screen instead of hiding its title and close button above the top edge. [Freebuff]
