# Izuki 1.0.116: real lessons, honest missions, a steadier Discord

## 🎓 Learn CMD or PowerShell, for real
"Teach me how to code on CMD" used to open Command Prompt, type `dir`, and stop. Every command Izuki wanted to show you was blocked, because it could only type commands you had spelled out yourself. Now it's a proper lesson:
- **A practice window opens** in a safe practice folder, `IzukiPractice` in your user folder. Nothing outside it is touched.
- **One idea per step:** what CMD is, `cd`, `dir`, `mkdir`, `cd` into a folder, `echo` into a file, `type`, `cls`, and then your first script, a `.bat` file with a variable and an if.
- **Every command really runs** in that folder. You see its real output, and Izuki explains what actually came back. A wrong command shows CMD's own error message.
- **You practise in the window.** "Check my work" looks in the folder to see whether you really did the exercise.
- **Say "next", "explain again", "let me try", "give me a challenge", "where are we", "start over" or "stop the lesson".** The lesson remembers where you are, by voice or typing, from the phone and Discord too.
- **PowerShell has its own lesson:** "teach me PowerShell".
- **When Izuki teaches on screen**, it may now show harmless basics (`cls`, `cd`, `echo`, `mkdir` of a plain new folder, `git status`). Anything that deletes, overwrites, chains or redirects still needs you to spell it out.

## ✅ Missions say what really happened
- **"Done" only means verified.** Missions are marked **Verified**, **Partly done**, **Blocked**, **Failed**, **Not verified** or **Needs you**, and the wording of a reply can never mark one done.
- **An email search in a mission is Verified** only when every linked Gmail account was searched.
- **Restarts never replay a mission.** One that was interrupted waits for you to check it, because it may already have sent or changed something.
- **Each mission has a saved goal and limits** on time, steps and app requests. You can see them under "What Izuki may do, and what it did".
- **The PC stays awake while missions run**, so it doesn't fall asleep halfway through while you're out. The screen still turns off and locks on your normal schedule. Normal sleep returns as soon as they finish.

## 💬 Discord stays connected
- **No more long silent gaps:** a stuck connection gives up after 20 seconds and tries again, waiting a little longer each time, up to a minute.
- **Nothing is lost after a drop:** Izuki picks the session up where it left off, and Discord sends the messages that arrived in the meantime.
- **Each message is answered once**, even if Discord delivers it twice around a reconnect.

## ✌️ Air control scrolls sideways
Peace sign ✌️, then move your hand **left or right** to scroll sideways: wide pages, carousels, timelines, the Start menu's pinned pages. Up and down still scrolls up and down. Izuki goes the way your hand clearly moves first, and sticks to it until you drop the peace sign, so a wobble never scrolls diagonally.

## 🏝️ The Island shows missions and lessons
While missions run, the Island shows the one being done and how many are left ("Missions · check my inbox +2"). During a lesson, it shows the step you're on ("CMD lesson · step 3 of 9 · mkdir"). Both show only while they're actually happening.
