# Izuki 1.0.38

Nova Notes: study notes from anything on your screen, with flashcards and quizzes.

## Nova Notes

- **"Take notes on this"** (or "save this to my notes", "jot this down") on
  any page, PDF, lab or quiz: Izuki reads the screen — the page's own text,
  or the screen's words when it's a PDF or picture — and writes clean study
  notes: a summary, the key points, key terms, and any question shown.
- A new **Notes** tab keeps them all on your PC, with search.
- Every note can become **Flashcards** (tap to flip), **Quiz me** (one
  question at a time), a **Summary**, or you can **ask about it**.
- **Teacher mode keeps notes by itself:** every question it explains goes
  into that day's lesson note for the page — ready to study later.
- The Island suggests **📝 Save notes from this** on course sites and PDFs.
