# Izuki 1.0.52

The 3D faces on the phone and the TV page now move as smoothly as on the PC.

## Phone
- The 3D avatar and hologram bust run at a steady 30 fps even when Izuki is resting. Blinks and breathing no longer stutter.
- Tap the face and it reacts (a jolt, a blink and a grin), the same as on the PC.
- The face smiles while Izuki is talking.
- A change of hair, skin, beard or voice shows on the face straight away.

## Lighter on the battery
- The phone and TV pages no longer re-read the face's saved look on every frame.
