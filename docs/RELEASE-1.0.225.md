# Izuki 1.0.225

Izuki can answer text questions before you add an AI key. The free starter brain is now available on the PC, phone and VR app. Its free service has limits: when it asks you to wait, Izuki waits for the stated time instead of resting that brain for hours. For screenshots or faster replies, add a compatible provider in Settings.

Quiz help follows your chosen rule: Izuki can fill answers on practice quizzes, drills and self-tests. On graded quizzes, exams, or a page it cannot classify, it says the answer and explains why while you choose it. Submit, Turn in and Finish remain yours on either kind of page. “Coach me” and “don't answer” still keep the answer back.

The PC app and VR room have a shared field of light that responds to listening, thinking, speaking and work. It pauses when hidden and keeps a still frame with reduced motion.

The local router option in PC Settings is now called **9Router or OmniRoute**. It connects to a router you set up on that PC; it does not bundle provider accounts or make their services unlimited.

Developer handoff notes now include the completed Graphify setup, a simple USB/new-PC guide, and the corrected next priority: Think with my PC. That feature and a complete PC-migration tool are not part of this release.

The physical Quest checks and a live task through the PC's own extension window remain on the device checklist. Automated tests do not establish those hardware results.
