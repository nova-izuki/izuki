# Izuki 1.0.32

Izuki installs on Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV — free.

## Izuki on your TV

- **Free, no app store needed:** on the TV, install the free **Downloader**
  app, open it and type **nova-izuki.github.io/tv** — Izuki downloads; press
  Install and open it from the TV's home screen.
- It shows up on the Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV home screen with its
  own banner, and works with just the remote: bigger text and a bright
  highlight on whatever's selected.
- TVs without a touchscreen or microphone can install it too.
- Roku, Samsung or LG: Izuki on your PC or phone controls those over Wi-Fi
  ("open Netflix on the TV").

## Search

- Google can now show Izuki's logo next to the site in search results, and
  the screenshots are described so they can appear in Google Images.
