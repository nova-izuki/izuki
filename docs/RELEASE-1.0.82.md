# Izuki 1.0.82: the Island asks about anything, and does it for you

## Ask about your screen, not just web pages
Open the Island anywhere: in Word, a game launcher, Settings, an error box. **Ask about your screen** takes a fresh look at what's in front of you and answers right in the Island. On a web page it still reads the page, as before.

## "Do it for me"
Ask "what do I do here?" and Izuki gives you the steps. Under the answer there's now a **Do it for me** button. Izuki does those steps on your screen while you watch, with Stop on the Island the whole way.

## + for photos, files and screenshots
The new **+** next to the question box adds things to your question:
- **Photo or file:** pictures, plus text files like notes, code, CSV and JSON.
- **Screenshot of my screen:** a fresh look at the screen, taken when you ask.

Up to 4 at a time.

## Your Island chats are kept
Every Ask conversation is saved as a tab at the top of the panel. You can:
- tap a tab to pick up where you left off;
- tap **New** for a fresh one;
- open the 🕘 list to see them all, **pin** the ones to keep for good, or delete any.

**Continue in Chat** carries a conversation over to the Chat tab.
