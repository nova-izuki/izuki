# Izuki 1.0.62

## Make my PC fast — one button (Settings → PC Boost, or just say it)
- **Right away:**
  - Clears old temp files and browser caches (Chrome, Edge and Brave, when they're closed; never logins or history).
  - Clears graphics shader caches, crash dumps and Windows error-report leftovers.
  - Frees memory by trimming every background program that isn't on screen. The amount freed is measured.
  - Closes hogging background updaters.
- **Found, then removed with one more tap:**
  - **Bloatware:** preinstalled junk like Clipchamp, Solitaire, Microsoft News and the Office ads hub. Apps you might use, like To Do or the new Outlook, are listed but not ticked. Anything removed can be reinstalled from the Store.
  - **Apps slowing your startup:** Teams, Edge and Chrome auto-launch, game launchers and updaters. They're switched off the way Task Manager does it, and one tap turns them back on.
- **Deeper clean:** Windows asks for permission once, then Izuki clears Windows' own temp folder, old update downloads and the delivery-optimisation cache.
- **Never touched:** your files, the recycle bin, security software, drivers, sync apps like OneDrive, or Izuki.
- **"Keep my PC fast by itself":** when the PC lags it cleans and frees memory on its own, and it does a quiet tidy once a day.
- **Say it:** "make my PC fast", "deep clean my PC", "remove the bloatware".

## Pick a voice, get its character
- Every voice card shows its character's face. Picking a voice brings that character up; if you're using a 3D face, it switches to the matching one.
- **Atlas** (the Jarvis-style voice) is now an armored robot: a gunmetal helmet, glowing eye slits that blink and glance, a light-bar mouth that moves with the words, and a glowing chest core that pulses as he talks.
- **Motion with more snap:**
  - Gestures swing past and settle.
  - Bodies squash and stretch, with a wind-up before big moves.
  - Hair swings and catches up.
  - In Comic style, motion lines follow fast hands.
