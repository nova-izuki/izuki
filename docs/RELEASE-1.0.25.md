# Izuki 1.0.25

Izuki starts offering help before you ask.

## Smart suggestions

- **It notices what you're on** and offers the right one-tap help:
  - YouTube → "Explain this video", "Sum it up"
  - a quiz or homework question → "Explain this question" (it teaches with
    the pen; it doesn't just give the answer)
  - course sites (uCertify, Coursera, Udemy, Brightspace, Pearson, Quizlet…)
    → "Explain this like a teacher", "Quiz me on this"
  - email and chats → "Help me reply" (it shows you before sending)
  - an error box → "Fix this error"
  - code → "Explain this code", "Find the bug"; spreadsheets → "Explain this
    formula", "Make a chart"; shopping → "Is this a good deal?"
- **Open the Island** (push your mouse to the top of the screen) to see them
  under "Suggested for you". For the important ones (an error, a question, a
  video) the Island peeks out by itself for a few seconds — never twice for
  the same page, and never on sign-in or password pages.
- **Instant and private.** Plain rules on your own PC; no AI, nothing sent
  anywhere, so it works with any brain, free ones included.
- Don't want the peeks? **Talk → Suggest help by itself**.

## Phone app

- **Suggestions that fit the moment**: plan your day in the morning, a lunch
  idea at noon, a focus timer in the afternoon, dinner and a day recap in the
  evening, a wind-down and a wake-up reminder at night, something fun at the
  weekend, and your next reminder when it's coming up.
- **An Island on the phone too**: a live pill under the header while Izuki is
  listening, thinking or talking, or when a reminder is due within 30
  minutes (tap it to hear what's coming up).

## Website

- The download area shows the newest version, how fresh it is and its size,
  read live from the release, so it's never out of date. Android, iPhone and
  "any browser" sit in one tidy row.
