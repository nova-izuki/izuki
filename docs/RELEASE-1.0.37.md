# Izuki 1.0.37

Pick the app's colours, ask-first in the orb's chat bar, and a more lifelike face.

## App colours

- **Settings → App colours**: big swatches — **Auto** (changes with the
  time of day: golden mornings, ocean days, sunset evenings, violet nights),
  Nova, Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Rose, Gold, Graphite, or **your own colour**.
  The glow behind the glass changes at once.

## Ask first or Auto-run — in the orb's chat bar too

- The orb's chat bar has an **🛡️ Ask / ⚡ Auto** button, like Claude Code.
- On **Ask**, when something needs running, Izuki says exactly what it
  wants to do and waits: say or type **"allow"** (or "go ahead", "run it")
  and it runs, then tells you how it went. "No" cancels.
- On **Auto**, it runs straight away and still shows you the command.
- (From your phone, commands still never run on the PC this way.)

## A more lifelike Hologram face

- **Blinks like a person** — quick, at irregular moments, sometimes twice.
- **Real eyes** — a bright iris ring and a dark pupil; the eyes lead the
  head and glance around now and then.
- **A talking mouth** — opens and closes on syllables, rounds into an "o"
  when wide, widens for "ee", the upper lip lifts, and the head nods on
  emphasis.
