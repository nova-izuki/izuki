# Izuki 1.0.50

Two new 3D faces you can style like a game character (a 3D avatar and a hologram bust), a redesigned Roku screen with one-press install and automatic updates, and changing Izuki's look just by asking.

## Two new 3D faces
- Under **Settings → Voice orb**: **3D avatar** and **Hologram bust (3D)**. The original hologram face is still there too.
- A real 3D head, drawn live: it follows your mouse around the screen, blinks, talks with the voice (the jaw and lips move, you see the teeth), smiles with the mood, and the hair swings a beat behind when the head turns.
- **Auto** picks the face from the voice: a man for a male voice, a woman for a female voice. Or choose one.
- On the phone and the Android TV app too.

## Style it (Avatar studio)
- **Hair:** dreads, long dreads, box braids, afro, curly, high-top, mohawk, fade, buzz cut, short, pixie, bob, long, long waves, bun, ponytail, bald.
- **Hair colour, skin tone, eye colour:** natural shades, plus chrome and android skin, pink, blue and mint hair, and glowing cyber eyes.
- **Beards:** stubble, moustache, goatee, short or full beard.
- **Glasses:** round glasses or a glowing futuristic visor. Glowing cyber seams, and the light colour.
- **🎲 Surprise me** for a random look.

## Change it by asking
- *"Give yourself dreads and a beard"*, *"make your hair pink"*, *"put on a visor"*, *"take off your glasses"*, *"be a woman"*, *"change your orb to the 3D avatar"*. It changes at once, with no AI call.

## A new Roku screen
- **Home:** the time, the date, the weather, a greeting and *Try saying…* ideas that change every few seconds (press ✱ for more).
- **While Izuki works:** the orb or face moves to the corner, and what you said plus Izuki's answer show in big, easy-to-read captions. It goes back home by itself.
- **The 3D faces on the TV:** they talk while Izuki speaks and blink when idle.

## Install or update the TV in one press
- **Settings → Control my TV:** type your Roku's developer password once and press **Install on my TV**. No more downloading and uploading files.
- It shows which version is on the TV, and with *Keep it updated by itself* on, the TV channel updates whenever Izuki does.
