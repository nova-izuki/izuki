# Izuki 1.0.90: characters that move like a person

## A character instead of the orb
Settings → Look → **Characters that move**: pick one of the 35 characters. They're a look for Izuki, just like the orbs. They show up only when you ask something, in the same place, and leave when it's done. There's no circle, just them, standing on your screen.

## They move like a person
- **Hello:** a wave when you start talking.
- **Listening:** the head tilts and stays with you.
- **Thinking:** a hand on the chin.
- **Working:** they type on a tiny laptop while Izuki does the task.
- **Answering:** the mouth moves with the voice, and they talk with their hands (open palms, hand on chest, shrugs, pointing up) and nod.
- **Like a desktop buddy:** they walk over to what Izuki is about to click and point at it.
- **Done:** they cheer or give a thumbs up. If something went wrong, they shrug.
- **Music:** they dance.
- **While waiting:** now and then they stretch, look around, check their phone, sip coffee, tap a foot or check the time.
- **Your mouse:** their head follows it.

Every character got its own skeleton, found in its painting, so the picture really bends at the shoulders, elbows, hips and knees.

## Your controls
- Drag them anywhere.
- Scroll over them (or pull an edge) to zoom.
- **–** shrinks them into a small bubble; **×** stops.
- They start small.

## Optional pet
A cat or a puppy that follows them around, sits when they stop and hops when they cheer. It's off by default and can be turned on under the character list.
